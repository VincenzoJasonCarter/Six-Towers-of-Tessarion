# Proving Grounds: party simulation (6 heroes)

Every party of 6 subclasses, repeats allowed (8,008 parties) against four encounters at levels 3, 7, 10, 15, 20 fights each: 2,562,560 fights. Generated by `uv run arena/party.py`; [README.md](README.md) has the rules and assumptions.

**How to read it**

- Each encounter is tuned so the average party wins 50%.
- **Value** is the main number: the win rate of parties that include the subclass minus the win rate of parties that don't, in percentage points. +5 means bringing it makes a party about 5 points more likely to win. Around 0 is average. Because a party has 6 of the 11 subclasses, values are relative to the field.
- **Survives** is how often that character is still standing at the end. **Damage** and **Taken** are its share of the party's damage dealt and taken (17% is an even share).
- Every fight starts fresh with full resources, as in the one-on-one run.

## Encounters

One creature per hero (the boss alone), built like the gauntlet's benchmark foes, with HP and damage multiplied by the calibrated scale.

| Encounter | Creatures | L3 scale | L7 scale | L10 scale | L15 scale |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Warband | brute, brute, soldier, soldier, soldier, skirmisher | ×1.342 | ×2.304 | ×2.231 | ×3.063 |
| Ambush | skirmisher, skirmisher, skirmisher, sniper, sniper, caster | ×0.93 | ×1.342 | ×1.333 | ×1.506 |
| Mixed | brute, soldier, soldier, skirmisher, sniper, caster | ×0.92 | ×1.485 | ×1.422 | ×1.654 |
| Boss | boss | ×1.672 | ×2.223 | ×2.389 | ×4.13 |

- **Skirmishers** ignore the front rank and go for the lowest-AC hero. **Snipers** shoot the lowest-AC hero in range. **Casters** blast a 10-foot radius wherever the most heroes stand. The **Boss** has three attacks, 10-foot reach and two Legendary Resistances (it shrugs off a save against Hold or a big spell).
- Base numbers per level: L3 CR 1 (AC 13, +4, DC 12); L7 CR 3 (AC 14, +5, DC 13); L10 CR 5 (AC 15, +6, DC 14); L15 CR 8 (AC 16, +7, DC 15).
