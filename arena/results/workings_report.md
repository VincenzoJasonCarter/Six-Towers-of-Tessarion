# Proving Grounds: the Common Workings

What the Aether Hall's general spells (`lore-book/27-common-workings.md`, modelled in `heroes/workings.py`) add to a Crystal Mage. Every four-hero party containing the mage (120 parties) against the four party encounters at levels 3, 7, 10, 15, 12 fights each, once as written and once with that one mage also knowing every working (same seeds). Generated by `uv run arena/workings.py`.

**How to read it**

- **Uplift** is the change in the party's win rate, in percentage points, from that one mage knowing the workings. Encounters are tuned so the average party wins 50%.
- **Bare**: floor, walls and ceiling only. **Furnished**: also loose objects, liquid, a rug and something hanging overhead, so Throw the Book, Flash Boil, Rug Pull and Assessed Weight work too.
- **Impact** is the mage's own contribution in HP per fight (damage + protection + control + enable + tanking, as in `party_report.md`).
- Noise: treat differences under about 1.5 points as noise.
