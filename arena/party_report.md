# Proving Grounds: party simulation (4 heroes)

Every party of 4 different subclasses (330 parties) against four encounters at levels 3, 7, 10, 15, 100 fights each: 528,000 fights. Generated by `uv run arena/party.py`; [README.md](README.md) has the rules and assumptions.

**How to read it**

- Each encounter is tuned so the average party wins 50%.
- **Impact** is the main number: the subclass's share of everything its party did in a fight, averaged over every party it is in (25% is an even share). Everything is counted in hit points, so the parts add up without weights:
  - **Damage**: damage it took off enemies' hit points (overkill doesn't count).
  - **Protection**: damage it kept off its allies: AC auras, Living Wall, Verdant Veil, Runic Bulwark on others, Intercepting Guard, Mana Cage, save bonuses and rerolls, temporary HP and healing it gave.
  - **Control**: enemy damage it denied: a held or stunned enemy's lost turn, a restrained one spending its action to escape, a slowed, rooted or frightened one that can't reach its target, a silenced caster, and attacks at disadvantage from its debuffs.
  - **Enable**: extra damage its allies dealt because of it: advantage against enemies it held or restrained, auto-crits on held enemies, lowered AC, worse saves.
  - Rolls are credited by expectation (the change in the chance to hit or save times the damage at stake), and a lost turn at the enemy's expected damage per round. Helping yourself (your own *shield*, your own advantage) is not credited; it shows in your Damage and Survives instead.
- **Win value** is the older measure: the win rate of parties that include the subclass minus the win rate of parties that don't, in percentage points. It captures everything, but doesn't say why, and it flattens out where parties win or lose regardless.
- **Survives** is how often that character is still standing at the end. **Taken** is its share of the damage the party took (25% is an even share).
- Every fight starts fresh with full resources, as in the one-on-one run.
- Bots and arena: current (area spells aimed off-centre, staggered formation, monsters judge targets by armour, Legendary Resistance refunds Hold tries; see README.md).

## Encounters

One creature per hero (the boss alone), built like the gauntlet's benchmark foes, with HP and damage multiplied by the calibrated scale.

| Encounter | Creatures | L3 scale | L7 scale | L10 scale | L15 scale |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Warband | brute, soldier, soldier, skirmisher | ×1.352 | ×2.652 | ×2.459 | ×2.998 |
| Ambush | skirmisher, skirmisher, sniper, caster | ×0.999 | ×1.528 | ×1.534 | ×1.648 |
| Mixed | brute, soldier, sniper, caster | ×0.999 | ×1.672 | ×1.523 | ×1.69 |
| Boss | boss | ×1.113 | ×1.501 | ×1.474 | ×2.441 |

- **Skirmishers** ignore the front rank and go for the lowest-AC hero. **Snipers** shoot the lowest-AC hero in range. **Casters** blast a 10-foot radius wherever the most heroes stand. The **Boss** has three attacks, 10-foot reach and two Legendary Resistances (it shrugs off a save against Hold or a big spell).
- Base numbers per level: L3 CR 1 (AC 13, +4, DC 12); L7 CR 3 (AC 14, +5, DC 13); L10 CR 5 (AC 15, +6, DC 14); L15 CR 8 (AC 16, +7, DC 15).
