## **I. General Description**

The **Meridian Spire**, often referred to in early texts as the Central Spire, is an ancient structure embedded beneath ██████████████████ and extending into the surrounding substrata of the Hexaspire basin.

Archaeological consensus holds that the Spire predates all permanent settlements in the region. Carbon scoring, stone memory analysis, and comparative resonance studies confirm an age that exceeds even the earliest elven chronologies by a margin now considered ██████████████████████████████████████████.

Despite its age, the Spire exhibits no visible signs of decay, collapse, or hostile activity. This durability has long been cited as evidence of its **passive design philosophy**, distinguishing it from the more visibly active Outer Towers.

## **II. Historical Context and Early Settlement**

Prior to the founding of Crownweave, the Hexaspire basin was described in fragmentary travelogues as “unsettled,” “resistant,” or “impractical for lasting habitation.” These conditions were attributed to unstable ley convergence and environmental unpredictability.

The establishment of early human encampments coincided with a notable reduction in such phenomena, a change now widely credited to the Meridian Spire’s latent influence.

Several pre-Charter journals speculate on intentional design or custodial presence during this period; however, these interpretations rely on sources later deemed ██████████████████████████████████████████ and are therefore excluded from modern curricula.

## **III. Relationship to the Outer Towers**

The Meridian Spire is traditionally understood as distinct from, yet complementary to, the Five Outer Towers.

Where the Outer Towers exhibit strong racial and elemental alignment, the Meridian Spire is characterized by **non-specific resonance**. It does not generate harvestable Soulstone, nor does it appear to favor any singular magical discipline.

Scholars describe its function using terms such as:

* “Dampener”
