The Six Towers do not exist independently. All available evidence indicates that they form a closed, interdependent system. Five are positioned along the world’s periphery, each associated with a dominant resonance axis observable across environmental behavior, biological tolerance, and long-term cultural patterning. This configuration is not symbolic, theological, or cartographic convenience. ## **IV. Resonance as Persistent Pressure**

Tower influence does not manifest as discrete magical output. It is continuous, ambient, and invariant with respect to attention, ritual, or use. For analytical purposes, resonance is best understood as **persistent ontological pressure** acting upon reality.

This pressure does not dissipate. It cannot be exhausted. Shielding, grounding, and dispersal techniques mitigate acute exposure but do not reduce ambient intensity. Long-term residence within a resonance field produces cumulative effects across multiple domains, including environmental deformation, biological tolerance shift, and cognitive alignment.

Environmental features exhibit altered mana density and stable anomalies. Biological organisms demonstrate changes in survivability thresholds rather than resistance. Cognitive systems—particularly among sentient populations—show gradual alignment toward perceptual and evaluative frameworks correlated with dominant local resonance. These alignments are not uniform and cannot be reversed without ████████████████████████████████.

The Towers do not persuade. They delimit.

## **VI. Mortality Density and Resonant Saturation**

One of the most consequential secondary effects of sustained Tower pressure is the accumulation of residual soul-energy in regions of repeated, resonance-aligned mortality. Where death occurs persistently under a dominant resonance axis, unanchored soul residue saturates subsurface strata.

Given sufficient duration and geological containment, this saturation crystallizes into mana-conductive structures commonly referred to as *soulstone*. Soulstone formation requires three conditions: prolonged resonance exposure, repeated mortality events, and structural containment. Absence of any one condition results in dissipation rather than crystallization.

Soulstone is therefore neither natural mineral nor artificial construct. It is a delayed structural echo of Tower influence mediated through death. Its alignment properties, instability outside native contexts, and differential biological compatibility follow directly from this origin.
