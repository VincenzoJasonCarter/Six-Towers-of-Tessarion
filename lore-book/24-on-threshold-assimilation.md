## **Abstract**

This paper documents a discontinuity in the development of practitioners who have undergone six Crystallizations. At this threshold the practitioner's body stops behaving as a passive recipient of absorbed mana and begins to behave as a site of soulstone formation. The process is the one by which soulstone forms beneath the Towers. Only the container differs.

A host at threshold cannot remain there. It settles into one of two stable states. In the **Vessel** state, containment seals and crystallization proceeds inside the body. In the **Conduit** state, absorbed mana is passed outward faster than it can settle. Common usage calls these states *Corrupted* and *Ascendant*. Both names are moral judgements and neither is accurate, but Section 4 retains them for consistency with field reports.

## **1\. Introduction**

Every practitioner knows the Moment of Crystallization: the fevered long rest after a decisive fight, the memory-echo of the dead whose souls fed the crystal, the waking to sharper abilities and slightly greener eyes. Instructors describe the process as cumulative, each Crystallization deepening the last by a small and comparable amount.

Field records do not support this. Across ███ documented careers, the sixth Crystallization differs in kind from the five before it. Reports converge on three observations:

1. The memory-echo does not end on waking. A second presence remains at the edge of attention, and in later reports it begins to respond.
2. The practitioner's output becomes unstable for several days, swinging well above and below its prior range.
3. Within ██ days the instability resolves, always in one of two directions, and the practitioner's abilities are afterwards altered in a way specific to their path.

Earlier accounts attributed this to divine favour, infernal bargain, or the "awakening" of a dormant gift. Each fails the test that *On the Phenomenon of Spire Confluence* applied to artifacts: the event is consistent across paths whose metaphysical framings share nothing. Verdant and Hellglass practitioners do not share gods, doctrines or teachers, and yet they cross the same threshold at the same point.

### **1.1 Scope of Study**

This paper addresses the conditions that produce the threshold, the two stable states that follow it, and the progression observed in the first of those states.

It does not address the condition of hosts beyond Index VI, the Arcanum's █████████ programme, or the ███████ hosts recovered from the Obsidian Crown frontier.

## **2\. The Host as Containment**

### **2.1 The Three Conditions**

*Regarding the Towers* (§VI) establishes that soulstone forms only where three conditions hold together: prolonged resonance exposure, repeated mortality, and structural containment. Without any one of them, soul residue dissipates instead of crystallizing.

A practitioner in active service satisfies all three.

* **Resonance exposure.** Attunement places the body in continuous contact with one crystal's resonance axis. Work inside a Tower's influence radius multiplies it.
* **Repeated mortality.** Every soulstone-touched creature the practitioner kills releases its stored mana, and the practitioner absorbs it. This is the mechanism of Crystallization itself. Over a career, the body becomes a site of repeated death.
* **Structural containment.** The body is a closed vessel. Absorbed mana enters it and does not, under ordinary conditions, leave.

Beneath the Towers these conditions take centuries and a mountain of rock. In a living host they are compressed into a single career. The sixth Crystallization marks the point at which internal saturation reaches the formation threshold (Figure 1, withheld).

The practitioner is not granted power at the threshold. The practitioner's body begins, very slowly, to become soulstone.

### **2.2 The Second Presence**

The presence reported at threshold is consistent with this model. Soulstone formed beneath the Towers keeps echoes of the dead whose residue composes it; this is why Verdant stone carries memory and Crimson Warshard carries rage. A host in early formation holds residue from every creature it has killed. We read the second presence as that residue beginning to cohere.

Practitioners first describe it as a crowd. Hosts in the Vessel state past Index III describe it, without exception, as a single voice.

### **2.3 Non-Attuned Hosts**

It was assumed that the Aegisbound, whose bodies left the neutral crystal dull at the Age-20 Rite, would be exempt. They are not. Exposure and mortality are indifferent to attunement. In Aegisbound hosts the containment is the armour: plates of soulstone dust and blood-alloy hold what the body cannot. The threshold arrives on schedule, and it arrives in the armour first.

The Range orders show the same displacement. Their crystal is carried in quiver and casing rather than in the body, and the threshold appears first in the munitions: arrowheads that hum in the dark, rounds that stay warm long after they were cast.

In both cases, the state the host settles into decides whether the formation stays in the equipment or crosses into the flesh.

## **3\. The Two Stable States**

### **3.1 Why the Threshold Cannot Hold**

*Regarding the Towers* (§V) observes that the Towers define a range of viable behaviours without prescribing any one of them. A host at threshold lies outside that range. Its saturation is too high to persist as an ordinary practitioner and too low to complete as stone. The condition behaves as an unstable equilibrium, and like every such equilibrium recorded in Tower systems, it collapses toward a stable state on one side or the other.

There are two. Every observed host has reached one of them. ███ hosts are recorded as having reached neither; their files are withheld.

### **3.2 The Vessel State (common usage: Corrupted)**

In the Vessel state the containment seals. Absorbed mana settles and accumulates inside the host, and the host's output rises sharply, because the host can now draw on the forming stone directly. The effect is strongest in short bursts of exertion, which field reports describe as the crystal "coming through."

Each such draw advances the formation by a measurable step. The Arcanum tracks this on the **Assimilation Index**, a six-stage scale. Rest reverses the Index slowly, about one stage per full night of non-exertion, and only while the host refrains from drawing on the stone. A host that draws more often than it rests advances.

**Table 1\. The Assimilation Index**

| Index | Observed condition |
| :---: | :---- |
| 0–I | No outward change. The second presence is audible during exertion only. |
| II | **Marking.** The physical signs catalogued for the host's crystal appear in full and stop fading between uses: leaf-veins under the skin, scars lit from within, stone-rough hands, smoke that clings, prismatic veins. |
| III | The second presence speaks outside exertion. |
| IV | **Fracture.** The long-term side effects catalogued for each crystal stop being tendencies and become constraints. The Verdant host cannot feel the urgency of short-lived things. The Warbound host cannot stop when the fight does. The Stonewarden host cannot change a plan once made. Fracture recedes with rest. |
| V | ████████████████████████████████████████████ |
| VI | **Loss.** During exertion, the second presence acts through the host. See Section 5\. |

The Aegisbound Vessel is the most visible case. From Index IV the armour can no longer be removed, and in several cases the seams between plate and wearer can no longer be found.

### **3.3 The Conduit State (common usage: Ascendant)**

In the Conduit state the containment does not seal. Absorbed mana passes through the host and out of it, into the creatures nearest it, faster than it can settle. The host's own output does not rise. What rises is the condition of everyone standing close by: wards reach further, wounds close, the next blow lands truer.

This is the mechanism that *Regarding the Towers* (§VIII and §IX) attributes to human institutions. Pressure is not removed but redistributed, spread across more bodies and more time so that no single point saturates. The Conduit is that mechanism at the scale of one person. The original manuscript carries a marginal note that the Conduits have adopted as a motto:

> *"The system does not require understanding. It requires throughput."*

Conduit hosts do not advance on the Assimilation Index. They do not mark, they do not fracture, and no Conduit has reached Loss.

The Conduit state is not reached by accident. Every recorded Conduit underwent, at or near threshold, the rite of **Entry** at an Aether Hall linked to the Meridian Spire. In the rite, the host's absorbed mana is recorded on the Hall's Throughput Roll as a standing obligation. How a ledger entry alters a body's containment is not understood. It is, however, reliable. Its single documented failure followed the ████████████ of the Roll concerned.

### **3.4 Movement Between States**

The two states are not symmetric.

A Conduit can become a Vessel. The host need only stop passing the mana outward and begin to draw on it, whether under duress, in isolation, or by choice. The Throughput Roll then records the obligation as defaulted.

A Vessel has never become a Conduit. Entry performed on a Vessel host has failed in all ██ recorded attempts. In ███ of them, the host's Index advanced by one stage during the rite.

No Prismatic Vessel past Index III has been available for interview.

## **5\. Loss**

At Index VI the formation is functionally complete. The host survives, eats, sleeps and answers to its name. During exertion, however, the second presence acts through the host, and what it does then is not what the host would have done. Observers in the Warbound and Sanguine cases describe the host continuing to fight after every enemy had fallen.

Loss is not permanent. Hosts have been recovered through a rite performed at an Aether Hall, which the Registry designates ██████████. Recovery has never returned a host below Index III. A recovered host is therefore always closer to the next Loss than to the threshold.

What becomes of a host that is not recovered lies outside the scope of this paper (§1.1).

## **6\. Concluding Statements and Withheld Projections**

Threshold Assimilation is not a corruption of the practitioner. It is the practitioner finishing what the Towers began. Given exposure, mortality and containment, formation follows; the host's only choice is whether to contain it or to pass it on.

Two projections follow from this, and both have been withheld in part.

The first concerns supply. If a single career can compress centuries of formation into one body, then soulstone has a second source that requires no mining. Section ███ of this paper, which examined that implication with reference to the Sangrith, was withdrawn at the author's request.

The second concerns the Throughput Roll. If every Conduit's absorbed mana is recorded as an obligation, then the Aether Halls hold, in aggregate, an outstanding debt of ██████████████████████. To whom it is owed, and when it falls due, the Roll does not say.

A note in the reviewer's hand appears below the final paragraph:

> *"We are not the miners. We are the seam." — V.H.*
