# Crystals

## **The Age-20 Rite (Aether Test & Choosing)**

* At **20**, every human in Tessarion is brought (or summoned) to a local **Aether Hall** linked to the Meridian Spire.

* They are tested with a **neutral aether crystal**:

  * If the crystal **remains dull** → their body cannot adapt to soulstone.

    * They can never become crystal mages.  
    * Many of these become **Aegisbound**, soldiers, craftsmen, merchants, etc.

  * If the crystal **glows** (even faintly) → they have **crystal affinity**.  
    * They are then allowed to **choose (or be assigned)** a path and travel to that region’s crystal to attune.

> In most cultures, “glow” at 20 \= both a blessing and a sentence. It opens doors, but it also marks you for politics, war, and tower business.

Once affinity is confirmed, they attune to **one** of the five main regional crystals (plus the optional human Aether if allowed):

---

## **1\. Verdant Soulstone (Elven Green)**

![][image1]

**Name(s):** Verdant Crystal, Thal’veren Soulstone  
 **Color:** Green / teal, faintly bioluminescent  
 **Source:** Forests of **Thal’vireth**, under the Verdant Tower. Formed from elven dead.

### **Typical Powers**

* Nature manipulation: roots, vines, terrain shaping.

* Illusions with light, foliage, and sound.

* Heightened senses and reflexes, subtle time-sense (anticipation, déjà vu).

* Reading emotional “echoes” in old places or objects.

### **Side Effects**

**Short-term / mild (early years):**

* Heightened sensitivity: strong reactions to noise, crowds, iron.

* Vivid, hyper-detailed memories of recent events.

* Plants seem to “respond” to your presence (turning, growing slightly faster).

**Long-term / deep attunement:**

* Emotional detachment: you start to see non-elves and short-lived beings as temporary and small.

* Time distortion: hours can feel like minutes when you focus; difficulty living in the present.

* Physical hints: faint leaf-vein patterns under the skin, eyes tinted green, slight scent of earth or rain.

## **2\. Crimson Warshard (Orc Red)**

![][image2]

**Name(s):** Warshard, Ironblood Crystal  
 **Color:** Deep red / ember-like glow  
 **Source:** Orc battlefields of the **Iron Hordes**, under the Red Tower. Formed from orc dead.

### **Typical Powers**

* Battle aura: boosts strength, speed, or damage in combat.

* Shockwaves, charges, explosive impacts.

* Self-sacrifice mechanics: trade your HP for temporary power.

* War cries: fear or frenzy effects in a radius.

### **Side Effects**

**Short-term / mild:**

* Adrenaline spikes, shaking hands after fights.

* Difficulty sleeping after combat; you replay battles in your head.

* You feel “itchy” or uncomfortable during long peace.

**Long-term / deep attunement:**

* Increased aggression, shorter fuse; you **want** conflict.

* The body craves extremes: combat, risk, intense stimuli.

* Physical signs: battle scars that glow faintly red under stress; heartbeat audible to those nearby during rage.

* Social risk: seen as unstable or dangerous in polite society.

---

## **3\. Amber Deepstone (Dwarven Gold)**

![][image3]

**Name(s):** Deepstone, Khazrund Amber  
 **Color:** Amber / gold, often faceted like cut gems  
 **Source:** Dwarven halls of **Khazrund**, under the Deep Tower. Formed from dwarven dead.

### **Typical Powers**

* Summoning protective barriers, shields, and walls.

* Inscribing runes for wards, traps, and buffs.

* Animating simple stone/metal constructs.

* Reinforcing armor, weapons, and even buildings.

### **Side Effects**

**Short-term / mild:**

* Strong preference for routines & structure.

* Discomfort when plans change suddenly.

* You start counting things (stairs, coins, doors) unconsciously.

**Long-term / deep attunement:**

* Mental rigidity: difficulty adapting to new ideas; “this is how it’s done.”

* Slower but deliberate reactions; you *hate* being rushed.

* Physical signs: skin texture subtly rough like stone, eyes amber-tinted, heavier gait.

* Social effect: excellent in crises (solid and calm), but frustrating in dynamic politics.

---

## **4\. Violet Hellglass (Tiefling Purple)**

![][image4]

**Name(s):** Hellglass, Emberfall Crystal  
 **Color:** Violet, with smoke-like swirls trapped inside  
 **Source:** Ashlands of **Emberfall**, under the Ashen Tower. Formed from tiefling dead.

### **Typical Powers**

* Curses and hexes: weaken enemies’ accuracy, strength, luck.  
* Shadow manipulation: short-range teleportation, concealment.  
* Pact magic: trade pain, memories, or time for power spikes.  
* Emotion / fear draining: feed off despair, guilt, or terror.

### **Side Effects**

**Short-term / mild:**

* Vivid, often unsettling dreams.

* Occasional whispers on the edge of hearing when using magic.

* Growing fascination with secrets, leverage, and people’s “true motives”.

**Long-term / deep attunement:**

* Paranoia: constant sense that there *is* a hidden agenda (often right).

* Temptation: you constantly see opportunities to exploit, blackmail, or bargain.

* Physical signs: eyes reflect candlelight too strongly, faint smell of smoke, shadows cling longer than they should.

* Metaphysical: things *may* whisper back; entities notice you as “someone who deals in debts.”

---

## **5\. Prismatic Omnicrystal (Sangrith Black)**

![][image5]

**Name(s):** Omnicrystal, Sanguine Prism  
 **Color:** Black with shifting prismatic veins, like oil on water  
 **Source:** Sangrith blood-alchemy near the **Obsidian Crown**, infused with blood from elves, orcs, dwarves, and tieflings.

> This path is rare, often illegal, and usually associated with Sangrith influence or very secret human sects.

### **Typical Powers**

* Hybrid magic: limited access to effects from all four other paths.

* Direct soul manipulation: lifedrain, forced healing, memory tampering.

* Corrupting or overcharging other crystals (muting them, shorting them out, or pushing them to dangerous output).

* Summoning “echo constructs” of the dead—memories given temporary form.

### **Side Effects**

**Short-term / mild:**

* Nausea, migraines after casting.

* Strange mixed auras: plants react, shadows stir, metal hums, etc.

* People feel “off” around you without knowing why.

**Long-term / deep attunement (and it escalates fast):**

* Physical mutation: pale or marble-like skin, dark sclera, nails turning clawlike, faintly visible veins of prismatic light.

* Soul slippage: flashes of other people’s memories; trouble distinguishing your emotions from those you absorb.

* Addictive rush: strong craving to use your powers more, push them further.

* High risk of becoming a **Sangrith thrall** or losing your humanity entirely if you don’t take measures to purify or restrain yourself.

## **6\. White Aetherite (Human Clear) – *Optional “Balance” Path***

![][image6]

**Name(s):** Aetherite, Clearstone  
 **Color:** Clear or milky white, faint inner shimmer  
 **Source:** Mixed or “unclaimed” human and cross-race souls around the **Meridian Spire** and Hexaspire.

> Not always offered as a choice; some orders reserve it for those meant to serve as mediators, healers, or tower-linked custodians.

### **Typical Powers**

* Purification: cleanse corruption from crystals, creatures, places.

* Stabilization: dampen wild magic, suppress illusions and curses.

* Weak mimicry: copy a small piece of another path’s effect for a short time.

* Support: reduce mana costs, extend durations, or shield allies’ magical workings.

### **Side Effects**

**Short-term / mild:**

* Calmness, emotional evenness; harder to panic.

* Occasional sense of detachment during conflicts (“I’m watching this, not in it”).

* Dreams of white light, quiet, and distant tower silhouettes.

**Long-term / deep attunement:**

* Emotional flattening: joy and rage both feel muted; it’s harder to get excited or deeply angry.

* Memory smoothing: sharp emotional edges of memories blur; trauma hurts less, but so do best moments.

* Physical signs: slightly luminescent eyes in darkness, faint halo-like shimmer around silhouette under strong mana.

* Social effect: trusted in crises, but might feel alien or “cold” to friends and family.

## **What Happens If You Don’t Choose?**

In most human societies on Tessarion:

* If you’re **affinity-positive** at 20 and refuse to choose:

  * Some factions will push you hard—governments, churches, or tower orders don’t like wasted potential.

  * You might be pressured into becoming:

    * A **non-attuned Aegisbound** (armor user with minimal crystal exposure), or

    * A bureaucrat / scribe in magic-adjacent jobs.

* If you never attune:

  * You remain magically “loud” to crystals (they feel you), but you never form a path.

  * Sangrith and certain cults might see you as *raw material*.
