# Leveling Up

## **✨ Ascending the Spire: Leveling Up in Tessarion**

In Tessarion, gaining a level is not merely an accumulation of skills; it is a profound crystallization of your soul's bond with the magical energy of the fallen. Whether you walk the path of the Mage (wielding raw Soulstone) or the Aegisbound (channeling it through armor), your progress is measured by how deeply the crystal's essence integrates with your being.

### **The Spark of Change: Requirements**

You advance by proving your capacity to handle the world's most volatile resources:

* **Defeating Monsters (Mana Accumulation):** Every creature you defeat that is touched by the Soulstone—whether a Coin-Mad Guard or a Prismatic Echo—releases its stored mana. Absorbing this excess energy forces your body to adapt and strengthens your crystalline pathways.  
* **Completing Quests (Attunement Focus):** Successfully navigating the political currents, deciphering the ancient runes in the Towers, or retrieving a vital piece of Blood Coin allows you to mentally anchor your powers. This focused effort ensures your growing affinity doesn't lead to the chaotic Mana Sickness that plagues the untrained.

### **The Moment of Crystallization**

When you reach the necessary experience to advance, you don't just "train" or "study"—you undergo a moment of profound, sometimes painful, attunement.

As you take a long rest after a pivotal fight or mission, the world seems to *thrum* around you. The sound of Hexaspire's subterranean vaults, or the wind rustling the boughs of Thal'veren, becomes impossibly sharp. You feel the presence of your Soulstone—be it the Verdant in your eyes, the Crimson in your heart, or the Sanguine in your veins—pulse like a fever.

You experience a flash of **memory-echo**: a fleeting, hyper-vivid vision of the lives that fueled your crystal's power.

* A **Verdant Mage** might see a split-second of an ancient elven warrior's last stand, granting them an increased **time-sense** (new *Verdant Veil* uses).  
* A **Warbound Mage** might feel the adrenaline-spike of a dying orc's final charge, causing their own *Blood for Power* to surge more violently.  
* A **Sanguine Aegis** might hear the combined, chaotic whispers of the four races, manifesting as new channels to store **Blood Charges** in their armor.  
* A **Ranger** might suddenly experience the ancient trauma of the land where their crystal was formed, sharpening their instincts and enabling a deeper **attunement to their terrain or prey**.

The crystalline energy is not subtle; it is a **sentence** and a **blessing**. When you wake, the process is complete: your abilities are sharper, your spells are more potent, and the physical hints of your attunement (green eyes, amber skin, faint smoke) are slightly more pronounced.

### **The Path's Deeper Cost**

Leveling up confirms the Side Effects (from the prelude) are deepening:

* **Mages** feel the psychological weight of their path more heavily: the Warbound Mage's fuse shortens; the Hellbound Mage's paranoia sharpens; the Aether Mage's emotions flatten further.  
* **Aegisbound** fighters find their armor demanding more: the Sanguine Aegis feels a profound Hunger of the Armor, and the Bulwark Aegis finds their preference for Anchored Stance becoming mental rigidity.

This ensures that advancing is not simply good; it means you are closer to losing yourself to the crystallizing influence of the dead souls.
