# **💱 Economy of Tessarion**

### **Official Market Value, Currency Conversion, and Local Price Distortion**

Across Tessarion, **value is standardized—but price is never guaranteed**.  
 While currencies differ by origin, **all trade ultimately references a single economic baseline** enforced by guild authority rather than national borders.

---

## **I. Official Tessarion Market Value (OTMV)**

**Official Tessarion Market Value (OTMV)** is the universal pricing reference established and enforced by the **Merchant’s Guild of Tessarion** in cooperation with chartered mints and audit houses.

* OTMV is used by:  
  * The Merchant’s Guild  
  * Chartered trade houses  
  * Inter-city and inter-polity contracts  
  * Long-distance caravan trade  
* **All Crownsblood (CB) prices listed in this book are OTMV**

> **Important:**  
>  OTMV is a *reference standard*, not a guarantee of what a buyer will actually pay in local markets.

---

## **II. Currency Conversion Rates (Trade Units)**

While OTMV defines *price*, currencies still vary in **accepted value** depending on origin and backing.

All currencies are converted internally using **Trade Units (TU)** for accounting.

## **III. Where Conversion Happens**

### **1\. Merchant’s Guild & Charter Houses (Official Path)**

* **Authority:** Merchant’s Guild, Temple Auditors, Charter Clerks  
* **Rule:** *“All coin is weighed by record, not blood.”*  
* **Conversion:** Always uses **official TU rates**  
* **Fee:** 2–3% service charge

This is the **only place where OTMV is guaranteed to apply cleanly**.

---

## **IV. Market Types & Pricing Rules**

### **🏛 Merchant’s Guild Markets (Official)**

All purchases use **OTMV** with **standardized rounding**.

#### **🔹 Rounding Rule — Standard Guild Accounting**

1. Add all item costs using OTMV.  
2. Apply rounding:  
   * **Below 0.5 → round down**  
   * **0.5 or above → round up**
3. **Minimum charge:** every purchase costs **at least 1 CB**, whatever the rounded total.

**Examples:**

* 2.4 CB → **2 CB**  
* 2.5 CB → **3 CB**  
* 0.2 CB → **1 CB** *(minimum charge)*

> *Guild doctrine:*  
>  Volume, predictability, and ledger accuracy matter more than individual margins.

---

### **🏚 Local & Unofficial Markets**

*(Loft Markets, street stalls, frontier traders, Underweave outlets)*

Local merchants **reference OTMV but do not honor it**.

#### **🔺 Rounding Rule — Always Round Up**

1. Calculate total using OTMV.  
2. **Always round the final price upward**, regardless of decimal.

**Examples:**

* 2.1 CB → **3 CB**  
* 2.9 CB → **3 CB**

*Local reality:*  
 Risk, scarcity, and lack of protection are priced into every sale.
