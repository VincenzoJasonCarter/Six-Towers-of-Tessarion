# On Resonant Crafting

# **On Resonant Crafting — Revised**

*The Soulforge Codex, Supplemental Volume*

---

> *"Foundational crafting asks the world for nothing.* *Resonant crafting asks the dead for permission.* *The dead are not always generous."*

---

## **Preface: What Separates Resonant from Foundational**

Foundational crafting is labor. You give time, heat, and care — the world gives you an item.

Resonant crafting is a negotiation with dead souls.

When soulstone is introduced to an item, the crystallized memory of the fallen presses against whatever you are making. You do not control that pressure. You declare your *intention* — how you want the dead to align — and the crystal decides whether it agrees.

This distinction matters for one reason above all:

**In foundational crafting, failure costs materials and time. In resonant crafting, failure costs you something harder to measure.**

---

## **The Five Steps (Sequence Lock)**

The order is mandatory. Skipping or reordering any step invalidates the attempt entirely — and not cleanly.

1\. Complete the base item (Foundational Crafting)  
2\. Introduce the Soulstone (Binding)  
3\. Declare your Resonance Posture (replaces Intent Tags — see below)  
4\. Choose Power Rating  
5\. Roll Resonance and consult the Outcome Band

A marginal note from a veteran smith: *"Most disasters came from people who thought step three was optional. The crystal always assumes a posture. Better yours than its own."*

---

## **Step 2: Binding — Introducing the Soulstone**

Before any resonance work begins, soulstone must be physically bound to the item.

**Binding Requirements:**

* You must have the correct soulstone type for your intended Posture (see below).  
* Binding requires a DC 12 tool check (Smith's tools, or Jeweler's tools for fine work). This is *not* the Resonance roll — it is pure craft. Failure here means the stone is wasted and the base item survives, undamaged.  
* Binding takes 4 hours and cannot be interrupted without voiding the attempt.

**Mixed Binding (Advanced):** Some smiths attempt to bind two different soulstone types simultaneously. This is legal but increases Resonance DC by \+10 for the entire attempt.

---

## **Step 3: Resonance Posture**

This is the revised core of the system, replacing the old Intent Tags.

### **What Posture Actually Is**

When you bind soulstone to an item, you are not simply adding magic. You are asking the crystallized dead — the specific people whose lives became that crystal — to express themselves through your work.

**A Resonance Posture is your declaration of *how* you are asking them to participate.**

It is not a description of the item's function. It is a relationship between you, the dead, and the thing you are making. Two identical swords, bound with identical soulstone, can have entirely different outcomes based on the posture declared — because the dead respond to intent, not to shape.

---

### **The Five Postures**

You declare **one or two** Postures before rolling.

* **Single Posture:** Focused, direct, and dangerous. Deepens the expression of the chosen Posture but reduces Stability Threshold by 1 (see Outcome Bands). The dead hear you more clearly when you speak simply — but speaking simply to the dead is a form of exposure.  
* **Dual Posture:** Safer. Two declared directions create internal tension in the crystal that actually buffers against worst-case fracture outcomes. The item is less extreme in either direction but more stable.

---

#### **POSTURE I: PREDATORY**

*"I am asking the dead to hunt alongside me."*

You are invoking the aspect of soulstone that formed beneath battlefields — the moments of pursuit, of final desperation, of lives cut short mid-charge. You are asking those memories to activate *when prey is weakest.*

**Thematic Identity:** The crystal rewards finishing what has already begun. It has no patience for mercy or hesitation.

**Active Condition:** Target is below 50% HP, *or* is currently Frightened, Charmed, Restrained, Prone, or otherwise compromised.

**Compatible Crystal :** Amber Deepstone

**Permitted Modifiers (choose one per trigger, non-stacking):**

* \+1d4 damage against active-condition targets  
* On a killing blow, regain HP equal to your proficiency bonus  
* Once per turn, ignore resistance (not immunity) to the item's damage type against an active-condition target

**Off-Posture Penalty:** Against full-health, uncompromised targets, attack rolls take −2. The crystal is impatient; it does not want this fight.

**Fracture Identity — Hunger Without Hunger:** The crystal has stopped distinguishing between prey and wielder. Once per long rest, when the active condition is *not* met on your attack, you take 1d4 damage as the item turns inward. This cannot be suppressed, only accepted.	

**Single-Posture Intensification:**

* Active condition threshold rises to ≤65% HP  
* \+1d4 becomes \+1d6  
* Fracture damage increases to 1d6

---

#### **POSTURE II: ANCHORING**

*"I am asking the dead to refuse to move."*

You are invoking the aspect of soulstone formed beneath sieges, last stands, and the deliberate choice to stay when leaving was still possible. You are asking those memories to express resistance — to make the item a fixed point.

**Thematic Identity:** The crystal rewards stillness and commitment. It does not understand retreat.

**Active Condition:** You have not moved more than 15 feet total on your current turn, *or* you are actively maintaining a position (holding a doorway, covering a creature, bracing against forced movement).

**Compatible Crystal :** Amber Deepstone

**Permitted Modifiers (choose one per trigger, non-stacking):**

* Reduce incoming damage by 3 (minimum 1\) while active condition is met  
* \+2 to all saving throws against forced movement, teleportation, or knockdown  
* Grant an adjacent ally \+2 AC until the start of your next turn (reaction, once per round)

**Off-Posture Penalty:** After moving more than 15 feet on a turn, your speed is reduced by 10 until the end of your *next* turn. The crystal is confused by the motion.

**Fracture Identity — Rooted Mind:** Prolonged use of a fractured Anchoring item causes the crystal's preference for stillness to bleed inward. After any combat in which you used the item, make a DC 12 Wisdom save or suffer disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws until your next long rest. The fracture can be identified by a faint amber hum the item develops when you begin to move.

**Single-Posture Intensification:**

* Damage reduction increases to 5  
* Speed penalty after movement increases to −15  
* Fracture DC increases to 14

---

#### **POSTURE III: CONTROL**

*"I am asking the dead to hold the line I draw."*

You are invoking the aspect of soulstone formed beneath fortifications, choke-points, and the deliberate shaping of where others could and could not go. You are asking those memories to express territorial dominance — to make space itself an argument.

**Thematic Identity:** The crystal rewards awareness of position and punishes those who enter your space carelessly. It does not distinguish between enemies and allies, which is the source of its greatest danger.

**Active Condition:** A creature enters or starts its turn in a space you have designated (bonus action, 15-foot radius centered on a point within 30 feet, lasts 3 turns).

**Compatible Crystal :** Violet Hellglass

**Permitted Modifiers (choose one per trigger, non-stacking):**

* Affected creature's speed is reduced by 15 until the end of their turn  
* Affected creature takes −1 to attack rolls while within the designated space  
* When an affected creature attempts to cast a spell, they must succeed on a DC (8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your relevant ability modifier) Concentration check or the spell fails

**Off-Posture Penalty:** Allies who enter the designated space without your explicit acknowledgment (free action: point at them) also suffer −10 speed. The crystal does not care who they are.

**Fracture Identity — Indiscriminate Ground:** The designated space stops responding to your dismissal. Once a fractured Control item's zone is placed, it cannot be ended early — it always runs its full duration, and the ally penalty becomes −15 speed. You can only place one zone per combat.

**Single-Posture Intensification:**

* Speed reduction increases to −20  
* Designated space increases to 20-foot radius  
* Fracture ally penalty increases to −20 speed

---

#### **POSTURE IV: PRECISION**

*"I am asking the dead to remember exactly how it was done."*

You are invoking the aspect of soulstone formed beneath the repetition of craft, training, and execution — lives spent refining a single skill to its absolute limit. You are asking those memories to impose discipline on your work, to eliminate variance.

**Thematic Identity:** The crystal rewards consistency and punishes chaos. It has no tolerance for sloppy conditions — including your own.

**Active Condition:** You are not suffering disadvantage on the attack or check, AND your target is within the weapon's normal (not long) range, AND you have not moved more than 10 feet this turn.

**Compatible Crystal :** Verdant Soulstone

**Permitted Modifiers (choose one per trigger, non-stacking):**

* \+2 to attack rolls while active condition is met  
* Once per turn, reroll one damage die; you may take either result  
* Critical hit range expands by 1 (e.g., 19-20 becomes 18-20) while active condition is met

**Off-Posture Penalty:** If active condition is *not* met, −2 to attack rolls. The crystal refuses to perform sloppily; it would rather fail cleanly.

**Fracture Identity — The Perfect Miss:** When you miss an attack while the active condition is not met, take 1 psychic damage. The crystal is expressing its disappointment. This is worse than it sounds — it is always exactly 1, always unavoidable, and experienced as a distinct, cold clarity rather than pain.

**Single-Posture Intensification:**

* Attack bonus increases to \+3  
* Critical range expands by 2  
* Fracture psychic damage increases to 1d4

---

#### **POSTURE V: VOLATILE**

*"I am asking the dead to release everything at once."*

You are invoking the aspect of soulstone formed beneath explosions, collapses, and deaths that were not quiet — moments of sudden, total expenditure of life. You are asking those memories to express excess, to hold nothing back.

**Thematic Identity:** The crystal rewards commitment to a single overwhelming moment. It does not do sustained work. It cannot be moderated once it decides to go.

**Active Condition:** You declare an Overcharge (bonus action) before making an attack or casting a spell. You can Overcharge a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Compatible Crystal :** Prismatic Omicrystal

**Permitted Modifiers (all apply simultaneously when Overcharged):**

* The attack or spell deals \+2d6 bonus damage of the item's type  
* If the attack hits, the target must succeed on a Constitution save (DC 8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ relevant modifier) or be knocked back 10 feet  
* Burst and area effects increase their radius by 5 feet

**Recoil (mandatory, always):** After an Overcharge resolves (hit or miss), take 1d4 damage. This cannot be reduced, redirected, or prevented. The crystal spent something, and it spent part of you.

**Fracture Identity — Involuntary Release:** Once per combat, at a moment of the DM's choosing (typically when you take significant damage), the item Overcharges *without your consent*. The burst affects all creatures within 10 feet, including allies. The recoil becomes 2d4. The item looks normal until it happens.

**Single-Posture Intensification:**

* Bonus damage increases to \+3d6  
* Knockback increases to 20 feet  
* Recoil increases to 1d6  
* Fracture recoil increases to 3d4, radius increases to 15 feet

### **Dual Posture: Combining Two Declarations**

When you declare two Postures, the item carries both active conditions and both modifier menus. However:

* You may only trigger **one** Posture's modifiers per triggering event (your choice in the moment).  
* The Off-Posture penalties of *both* Postures apply independently.  
* Fracture identities, if reached, manifest for **both** — but at reduced severity (use the listed values, not Single-Posture intensification).  
* Dual Posture items have a \+1 to their effective Stability Threshold (see Outcome Bands).

**Design philosophy:** Dual Posture items are safer and more flexible, but the two Postures often work against each other in practice. A Predatory/Precision item wants you still (Precision) and targeting wounded enemies (Predatory) — which means you're waiting, not hunting, and the Off-Posture penalties compound if you move to chase.

## **Step 4: Power Rating**

Power Rating defines the ceiling of what you are asking the world to allow. No outcome, regardless of the Resonance roll, can exceed the ceiling set by Power Rating.

**Power Rating is chosen before you roll. It cannot be changed.**

| PR | Outcome Ceiling | Resonance DC |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 1 | Uncommon | 50 |
| 2 | Uncommon (Refined) | 55 |
| 3 | Rare | 65 |
| 4 | Epic | 80 |
|  |  |  |
|  |  |  |

The DC is not difficulty. It is permission. The world does not grade you on a curve.

## **Step 5: The Resonance Roll and Outcome Bands**

### **Building the Resonance Pool**

The Resonance roll is not a single die. It is a pool, assembled from contributing factors:

**Base Pool:**

* (PR \+ 1\) \* d20

**Modifiers (cumulative):**

| Factor | Modifier |
| ----- | ----- |
| Relevant tool proficiency | \+5 |
| Expert-grade tools (masterwork smith's kit, etc.) | \+3 |
| Crafting in a location with high ambient resonance (near a Tower, in a crystal vein) | \+5 to \+15 (DM's discretion) |
| Each previous successful resonant craft with this Posture | \+1 (max \+5) |
| Soulstone is native to the Posture declared (e.g., Crimson Warshard for Predatory) | \+5 |
| Soulstone is *off-native* but compatible | \+0 |
| Soulstone is actively hostile to the Posture (DM's discretion) | −10 |
| Single Posture declared | −5 (the exposure cost) |
| Mixed Binding (two soulstone types) | −10 |
| Crafter is currently suffering a Posture fracture effect | −5 |

### **The Outcome Bands**

Compare your final Resonance roll to the chosen Resonance DC.

| Roll vs DC | Outcome | Item Result |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| DC \+ 15 or more | **Resonant Apex** | Item reaches PR ceiling. Gains one *Echo Trait* (see below). |
| DC \+ 1 to \+14 | **Full Resonance** | Item reaches PR ceiling. Standard resonant item. |
| DC exactly | **Threshold** | Item reaches PR ceiling, but one Posture modifier is locked (DM chooses which). |
| DC − 1 to −9 | **Dim Resonance** | Item forms at one rarity tier *below* PR ceiling. Posture functions at half modifier values. |
| DC − 10 to −19 | **Fractured Emergence** | Item forms at two rarity tiers below PR ceiling. Fracture Identity is immediately active. |
| DC − 20 or lower | **Rejection** | The soulstone refuses entirely. Item reverts to its foundational state (no resonance). Crafter must make a DC 15 Constitution save or suffer one level of exhaustion. If the Posture was Single, they also gain the Fracture Identity effect for 24 hours from the residual crystal energy. |

**Stability Threshold** applies to Fractured Emergence: single-Posture items reach Fractured Emergence at DC − 8 (not DC − 10), because the exposure is higher. Dual-Posture items reach it at DC − 12\.

## **Rarity: What the Tiers Actually Mean**

This is what changes as an item climbs the Power Rating scale.

### **Uncommon (PR 1\)**

The Posture is *present but young.* The dead have answered, but quietly.

* One Posture modifier is available.  
* No Off-Posture penalty yet. The crystal is not strong enough to punish you.  
* Fracture Identity exists but is dormant — it will only activate if the item is pushed (e.g., via the Overcharge system, or if subjected to corrupting resonance).  
* Physical hint: faint warmth or slight discoloration when the Active Condition is met.

### **Uncommon Refined (PR 2\)**

The Posture has settled. The crystal remembers its purpose clearly.

* Two Posture modifiers available (choose one per trigger).  
* Off-Posture penalty now active.  
* Fracture Identity is live.  
* Physical hint: the item carries a faint ambient sound — a heartbeat, a whisper of wind, a low hum — that quickens when the Active Condition is met.

### **Rare (PR 3\)**

The dead in this crystal are *loud.* The item has started to develop preferences.

* All Posture modifiers available.  
* Off-Posture penalty increases by 1 (e.g., −2 becomes −3, speed penalties increase by 5).  
* Fracture Identity, if active, gains an additional minor effect specific to the crystal's origin race (e.g., a Rare Predatory item made from Warshard might also cause the wielder to hear the dying orc's last war cry when the fracture activates).  
* Physical hint: the item's appearance changes noticeably. Color bleeds into the material. Light bends near it. Ambient temperature shifts.

### **Epic (PR 4\)**

The item is no longer simply a tool that has resonance. It is a resonant object that resembles a tool.

* All Posture modifiers available, at Single-Posture Intensification values regardless of whether Single or Dual Posture was declared.  
* Off-Posture penalty increases by 2 total.  
* The item gains one permanent **Echo Trait** (see below).  
* Fracture Identity, if active, applies constantly rather than situationally.  
* Physical hint: the item is visibly, unmistakably not mundane. Carrying it openly in most cities requires explanation.

### **Legendary (PR 5\)**

The crystal has fully overtaken the material. The dead are present when you hold this object.

* All modifiers at Single-Posture Intensification plus one additional die step on all damage values.  
* The item communicates — not in words, but in pressure. You always know when the Active Condition is met without looking. You always know when the Off-Posture penalty is about to apply.  
* Two permanent Echo Traits.  
* Fracture Identity is a personality, not just a mechanic. The DM and player define jointly what the fracture *wants.*  
* **Attunement required.** The item cannot be loaned, dropped intentionally, or used by another creature without the resonance collapsing to Dim quality until re-attuned.

### **Mythic (PR 6\)**

There is a soul in here. It may not be a human one.

* All Legendary benefits.  
* The item has a name. It had one before you made it; the crafting revealed it.  
* Three permanent Echo Traits.  
* The Fracture Identity, if active, can affect others near the wielder — within 10 feet, creatures of the DM's choosing may occasionally experience the fracture effect.  
* **Requires dual attunement.** Two characters must attune to the item together. If one dies, the surviving attuned must make a DC 18 Wisdom save or immediately suffer the Fracture Identity effect.

## **Echo Traits**

Echo Traits are emergent properties that appear when an item resonates more strongly than expected (Resonant Apex outcome, or Epic+ rarity). They are *not* chosen by the crafter — they are generated by the DM based on the specific soulstone used and the circumstances of the craft.

The following table provides examples. The DM should invent freely within the theme.

| Posture | Example Echo Traits |
| ----- | ----- |
| Predatory | *The Hunt Remembers:* Once per day, the item marks a creature the wielder has previously reduced to 0 HP. The mark lasts 24 hours; the wielder knows the marked creature's direction and approximate distance. |
| Predatory | *Execution Hunger:* When the wielder reduces a creature to 0 HP, they may immediately make one additional attack with this item as a free action. |
| Anchoring | *Last Wall:* When the wielder is reduced to 0 HP while the Active Condition is met, they drop to 1 HP instead. Once per long rest. |
| Anchoring | *Weight of Refusal:* Any creature that attempts to forcibly move the wielder must first succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC 14\) or the attempt fails entirely. |
| Control | *Contested Ground:* Creatures that fail their save against the Control zone are additionally marked. Marked creatures grant the wielder \+1 to attack rolls against them for 1 minute. |
| Control | *The Space Between:* The designated zone becomes difficult terrain for enemies AND lightly obscures them (attacks against them from outside the zone have −1 to hit). |
| Precision | *Cold Certainty:* Once per turn, if the wielder's attack roll result is within 3 of the target's AC (a near-miss), they may reroll. They must use the second result. |
| Precision | *One Clean Line:* The item ignores cover bonuses when the Active Condition is met. |
| Volatile | *Chain Reaction:* When the Overcharge knockback moves a creature into another creature's space, the second creature also takes half the Overcharge bonus damage. |
| Volatile | *Spent and Burning:* After an Overcharge, the item glows until the wielder's next turn. Any creature that hits the wielder with a melee attack during this time takes 1d6 fire or necrotic damage (match the item's soulstone origin). |

## **Fracture Recovery**

A Fracture Identity does not have to be permanent.

**Suppression:** With access to an Aether Mage (or Aetherite of sufficient purity), a fractured item can be suppressed — the Fracture Identity becomes dormant again, as in an Uncommon item. This costs materials and time but not the item's other properties.

**Purification:** A full purification removes the Fracture Identity entirely but reduces the item's effective rarity by one tier. The dead whose memory was fracturing have been quieted; something left with them.

**Acceptance:** Some wielders choose neither. A fractured item carried knowingly, its flaw understood and incorporated into how the wielder fights, is considered by certain Tessarion traditions to be the most honest form of resonant equipment. There are no mechanical benefits to this. It is simply true.

## **Quick Reference: Resonant Crafting Checklist**

□ 1\. Complete foundational base item  
□ 2\. Bind soulstone (DC 12 tool check, 4 hours)  
□ 3\. Declare Resonance Posture (1 or 2\)  
     □ Single Posture: −5 to Resonance roll, deepened effects  
     □ Dual Posture: \+1 Stability Threshold, safer  
□ 4\. Choose Power Rating (1–6), sets Resonance DC  
□ 5\. Build Resonance Pool (2d20 take higher \+ modifiers)  
□ 6\. Compare roll to DC → consult Outcome Band  
□ 7\. Apply rarity tier properties  
□ 8\. If Apex or Epic+, determine Echo Trait with DM  
□ 9\. If Fractured, activate Fracture Identity
