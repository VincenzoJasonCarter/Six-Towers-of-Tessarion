# Common Workings

*A primer of the general workings, issued by the Aether Hall to every human whose crystal glows at the Age-20 Rite.*

The workings set down here belong to no single path. Whatever crystal you were attuned to, Verdant or Warshard, Deepstone or Hellglass, Omnicrystal or Aetherite, every one of them is a **wizard spell** that a Crystal Mage of any Arcane Tradition may learn. Most of the cantrips are the small forms of the greater workings that follow them; the Hall advises that the small form be learned first.

Soulstone is made of the dead, and a working borrows a little from whoever is in your crystal. The Hall reminds the newly glowing that the dead are, on the whole, helpful.

## Cantrips

### Melt Down

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** 1 minute

You touch a Tiny nonmagical object, or a Tiny part of a larger one (a nail, a hinge pin, a lock's bolt), that isn't being worn or carried. Its state of matter shifts one step: solid to liquid, or liquid to gas, or back. Its temperature doesn't change.

When the spell ends, the object returns to its original state in whatever shape it ended up in. A melted bolt that ran out of its lock hardens as a puddle of iron on the floor. A gas settles as a pinch of dust or a few droplets wherever it drifted.

You can have only one object affected at a time; casting the spell again ends the first. Soulstone and magic items are unaffected.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The object can be Small at 5th level, Medium at 11th level, and Large at 17th level.

***Margin.*** *"good locks have soulstone pins now. thanks a lot, everyone."*

### Slip & Grip

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of oil or a pinch of chalk)\
**Duration:** 1 minute

Choose a 5-foot square of surface you can see within range, and one setting:

- **Slick.** The square is difficult terrain. A creature that enters it while dashing, or is pushed into it, must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.
- **Grippy.** A creature can move across the square without a check, even up a wall or upside down along a ceiling, without using its hands.

You can switch the setting as an action. Casting the spell again ends the previous casting.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** You affect two squares at 5th level, three at 11th level, and four at 17th level. The squares must touch each other.

***Margin.*** *"used both settings at a wedding."*

### Harvest Minute

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a Tiny nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried by a creature other than you, and move it through time in one of two directions:

- **Forward.** Up to a year of ordinary time passes for the object at once. Fruit ripens or spoils, ink dries, a nail rusts, a rope frays, a seed sprouts.
- **Back.** Up to the last minute of change is undone. A spilled cup refills from the liquid on the floor, a snapped quill rejoins, a letter burned in the last minute comes back from its ashes. All the pieces must be within 5 feet of the object.

The spell can't affect creatures, magical effects, or soulstone.

***Margin.*** *"won't age promises."*

### Not Yet

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action, or 1 reaction, which you take when a Tiny object you can see within 30 feet of you falls, tips, or is dropped\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The object stops in time for the duration. It hangs where it is, a flame on it stops burning, and nothing on it decays or spills. A creature can use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC to move the object, which ends the spell.

The spell can't affect an object that is being worn or carried, or that is part of an attack (an arrow in flight, a thrown dagger).

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The object can be Small at 5th level, Medium at 11th level (a door mid-swing), and Large at 17th level (a falling cart).

***Margin.*** *"saved the soup. lost the argument."*

### Peephole

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** 1 minute

A 1-foot circle of a nonmagical wall, door, floor or ceiling you touch, up to 1 foot thick, becomes transparent from both sides. It stays solid and still blocks sound. You can end the spell early (no action required).

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The surface can be up to 2 feet thick at 5th level, 3 feet at 11th level, and 5 feet at 17th level.

***Margin.*** *"assessed at half a window."*

### Pocket Echo

*Illusion cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a Tiny object. Over the next minute it stores one sound of up to 6 seconds that is made within 10 feet of it: a voice, a cough, a door slamming. Your own voice counts.

Before the spell ends, you can use a bonus action to make the object play the sound back once, as long as you are within 120 feet of it. It plays at its original volume or as quiet as a whisper, your choice. The spell then ends. You can have only one echo stored at a time.

***Margin.*** *"mine adds a cough at the end. it is not my cough."*

### Teacup Everstorm

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

A small grey cloud appears over a 5-foot cube within range. Choose one weather; you can change it as an action:

- **Drizzle.** Puts out unprotected flames in the cube and soaks everything in it.
- **Gust.** Moves Tiny unattended objects in the cube up to 10 feet and blows out candles and torches.
- **Spark.** A single spark lights one flammable object of your choice in the cube (a fuse, a wick, a pile of tinder).

***Margin.*** *"it is not a real Everstorm. do not tell it."*

## Combat Cantrips

### Throw the Book

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose a loose, unattended nonmagical object weighing 1 to 20 pounds within 30 feet of you, and a creature within range. The object flies at the creature. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 bludgeoning damage. The object lands in the target's space or next to it, and breaks if it is fragile.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d10 when you reach 5th level (2d10), 11th level (3d10), and 17th level (4d10).

***Margin.*** *"in a library, by preference, a book. the librarians have stopped asking."*

### Flash Boil

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Choose at least a cupful of nonmagical liquid within range that isn't being worn or carried: a puddle, a mug, a barrel, a ditch. Up to a gallon of it bursts into scalding steam. Each creature within 5 feet of it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d8 fire damage. The area within 5 feet is lightly obscured until the start of your next turn.

**Cantrip Upgrade.** The damage increases by 1d8 when you reach 5th level (2d8), 11th level (3d8), and 17th level (4d8).

***Margin.*** *"Stormwake sailors call it making tea."*

### Rug Pull

*Transmutation cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You yank one of the following within range, up to 15 feet in a direction of your choice:

- a rug, tablecloth, sail, curtain or similar cloth no larger than a 15-foot square;
- a rope, chain or lever;
- any other loose, unattended object weighing up to 20 pounds.

Each creature standing on a yanked cloth must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. Anything resting on a yanked tablecloth falls off. A yanked lever is pulled.

***Margin.*** *"the tablecloth trick does not work. the falling-over trick does."*

## 1st Level

### Mind the Floor

*1st-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of oil or a pinch of chalk)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a 20-foot square of surface within range: floor, wall, ceiling or stair. Choose one setting; you can switch it as an action:

- **Slick.** The area is difficult terrain. A creature that enters the area or starts its turn there must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.
- **Grippy.** Any creature can move across the area, even up a wall or upside down along a ceiling, without a check and without using its hands.

**At Higher Levels.** Each side of the square increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 1st.

***Margin.*** *"Khazrund stair-wardens invented it. they will not say which setting came first."*

### Assessed Weight

*1st-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a gate-toll receipt)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

One nonmagical object you can see within range, up to Huge size and not worn or carried, weighs ten times as much or one tenth as much (your choice) for the duration.

- **Lighter.** It can be lifted, pushed or pulled as if it weighed a tenth as much. A cart can be drawn at double speed.
- **Heavier.** A door or gate held this way adds 5 to the DC of checks to force it. A floor or bridge might give way (DM's call). If the object falls onto a creature, the creature makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d10 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

**At Higher Levels.** You can target one additional object for each slot level above 1st.

***Margin.*** *"the toll gate weighs carts, not what's in them. you're welcome."*

### Put It Back

*1st-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

Up to twenty loose, unattended nonmagical objects within range, each weighing no more than 50 pounds, fly up to 30 feet and settle where you choose within range. You can stack them neatly: shelve a library, clear a doorway of rubble, or pile furniture into a barricade that gives half cover (three-quarters cover if it is big enough, at the DM's call). An object can't be thrown at a creature, and it stops short of an occupied space.

If you don't choose where the objects go, each one goes back to **where it belongs**: where it was kept, or where it last stood for a long time. The DM decides.

**At Higher Levels.** You can move ten additional objects for each slot level above 1st.

***Margin.*** *"it puts them back where IT thinks they go. useful at a crime scene. upsetting at home."*

### Hawker's Shell

*1st-level illusion*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a seashell or a cracked bell)\
**Duration:** Until dispelled

You touch an object and choose a command word. Over the next 10 minutes, the object records up to 1 minute of sound made within 30 feet of it. You can stop the recording early.

Afterward, you (as a bonus action, within 120 feet of the object) or anyone touching the object who speaks the command word can make it play the recording, at any volume from a whisper to a shout. It can be replayed any number of times.

Alternatively, you can release the recording all at once as a **thunderclap**, which ends the spell. Each creature within 10 feet of the object must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be deafened for 1 minute, and nonmagical glass and crystal within 10 feet shatters. Soulstone doesn't.

***Margin.*** *"every hawker on the Groundring has one. that is why it is so loud."*

### Banked Fire

*1st-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose two nonmagical objects or bodies of material within range, each no larger than a 10-foot cube and not worn or carried: a **source** and a **receiver**. For the duration, heat flows from the source into the receiver.

- **The source** goes cold. Water in it freezes solid enough to hold a Medium creature, iron frosts over, a fire in it goes out.
- **The receiver** heats up. Water boils, metal glows and softens enough to bend by hand (a DC 10 Strength check), and wood smoulders and catches fire at the end of the duration.

A creature that starts its turn touching or standing in a heated receiver takes 1d6 fire damage. The spell can't take heat from a creature or give heat to one. When the spell ends, both return to normal temperature at their natural rate.

***Margin.*** *"Emberweave kitchens chill the butter with the oven. nobody else has thought of it."*

## 2nd Level

### Harvest Hours

*2nd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a seed and a pinch of ash)\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch nonmagical material that fills no more than a 10-foot cube (an object, a section of wall, a stand of plants), not being worn or carried. Move it through time in one direction:

- **Forward.** Up to 100 years pass for it at once. Iron rusts (a rusted lock or chain breaks with a DC 10 Strength check), rope and wood rot, mortar crumbles, plants grow to full size, fruit ripens or rots.
- **Back.** Up to the last 24 hours of change is undone. A broken object reassembles, a burned letter is restored, spilled liquid returns to its container, rubble rises back into its wall. All the pieces must be within the cube.

The spell can't affect creatures, magic items, soulstone, or changes caused by magic.

**At Higher Levels.** With a 3rd-level slot, the limits become 1,000 years forward or 7 days back.

***Margin.*** *"the Ledger-keepers in Thal'vireth call the forward use theft from the Count. the back use, they don't talk about."*

### Payment Deferred

*2nd-level abjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when a creature or object you can see within 60 feet of you would take damage from falling, or bludgeoning damage from an impact that isn't a weapon attack or a spell (a collapse, a battering ram, a falling beam, being thrown into a wall)\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V\
**Duration:** 1 hour

The creature or object takes no damage from the impact. The force is stored in the surface or object it struck, which shows a faint ring.

Before the spell ends, while you are within 60 feet of the ring, you can use an action to release the force. Each creature within 10 feet of the ring makes a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, it takes 2d8 force damage and is pushed 15 feet away from the ring. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.

If you don't release the force before the spell ends, it releases on its own.

**At Higher Levels.** The force damage increases by 1d8 for each slot level above 2nd.

***Margin.*** *"the bill always comes. ask the man who fell off the Aerialring and then stood under it at the wrong time."*

### Window Tax

*2nd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a copper coin, which the spell consumes)\
**Duration:** 10 minutes

A 10-foot square of nonmagical wall, floor, ceiling or door you touch, up to 5 feet thick, becomes transparent from both sides. It stays solid and still blocks sound. You can end the spell early (no action required).

**At Higher Levels.** The surface can be 5 feet thicker for each slot level above 2nd.

***Margin.*** *"the Council ruled that glassed walls count as windows. for tax. the coin is the tax."*

### Fault Line

*2nd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a mason's chisel)\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch nonmagical stone, brick, earth, wood or ice, and it splits cleanly along its weakest seam. Choose one:

- **Passage.** A gap 5 feet wide and 8 feet tall opens through it, up to 5 feet deep. The rubble falls to either side. The structure stays standing unless what you split was holding something up (DM's call).
- **Trench.** The floor or ground opens into a trench 10 feet long, 5 feet wide and 5 feet deep. A creature standing over it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall in, taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage and landing prone.

The split is permanent and as loud as a cart crash; it can be heard 300 feet away. The spell can't affect soulstone, magical material, or Tower material.

**At Higher Levels.** The passage or trench can be 5 feet deeper for each slot level above 2nd.

***Margin.*** *"a Khazrund mason watched me cast this and told me, at length, where I went wrong."*

### Tide Clerk

*2nd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You take control of nonmagical liquid that fills no more than a 20-foot cube within range. Choose one of the following effects; you can switch to another as an action:

- **Part.** The liquid draws aside, leaving a dry path up to 10 feet wide through the area.
- **Wall.** The liquid rises into a wall up to 20 feet long, 10 feet high and 5 feet thick. Ranged weapon attacks through it have disadvantage, and creatures behind it take half damage from fire.
- **Wave.** The liquid surges in a direction you choose. Each creature in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and pushed 10 feet in that direction.
- **Flow.** The liquid moves where you direct it, uphill included, at up to 30 feet per round.

***Margin.*** *"Caer Veyn harbour clerks part the shallows to read the hull numbers. nobody believes this until they see it."*

### Thread Bridge

*2nd-level conjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a spool of thread)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

A cord of glowing thread stretches between two solid points you can see within range, up to 60 feet apart, and holds the shape you choose:

- **Bridge.** A walkway 5 feet wide that holds up to 2,000 pounds.
- **Rope.** A climbing rope.
- **Net.** A 10-foot square of tangled thread. The area is difficult terrain, and a creature in it when it appears, or that enters it, must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or be restrained. A restrained creature can use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC to free itself.
- **Tripwire.** A line at ankle height. The first creature to cross it unaware must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone, and the wire vanishes.

The thread has AC 12 and 15 hit points, and is vulnerable to fire and slashing damage. If it is reduced to 0 hit points, the spell ends.

**At Higher Levels.** The thread can be 30 feet longer for each slot level above 2nd.

***Margin.*** *"what is torn, we weave anew. what is not torn, we weave anyway."*

## 3rd Level

### Matter Conversion

*3rd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a drop of quicksilver in a stoppered vial)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You touch a nonmagical object, or one connected mass of material (a wall, a floor, a pond), and change its state of matter. You affect a section no larger than 30 feet by 30 feet and 5 feet thick. You can't target creatures or anything being worn or carried.

Change the material from solid, liquid or gas into either of the other two states.

- **Temperature.** The material's temperature doesn't change. Molten iron made this way is at room temperature and safe to touch, and ice made from a lake is not cold.
- **Liquid.** The material slumps and flows downhill. On level ground it spreads into a shallow pool that is difficult terrain. A wall turned to liquid collapses, and whatever it held up may fall.
- **Gas.** The material becomes a cool grey fog that fills its space and spreads 10 feet in every direction. The area is heavily obscured. A strong wind moves it.
- **Solid.** A liquid or gas turned solid holds the shape it had at that moment. You can walk on a pond, or walk on the surface of a fog bank.

**When the spell ends,** the material returns to its original state in its new shape and place. A wall that became a puddle hardens into a slab on the floor. Iron poured into a mould comes back as an iron bar. A gas condenses where it drifted: a lake turned to fog falls as rain, and stone turned to fog falls as gravel and dust.

**Caught creatures.** When liquid or gas around a creature turns solid, the creature makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a success, it scrambles to the nearest unoccupied space. On a failure, it is encased and restrained, though it can still breathe. It can use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC to break free.

Soulstone, magic items and Tower material can't be converted. The size limit doesn't increase with a higher spell slot.

***Margin.*** *"someone tried it on the Spire. the Spire did not notice."*

### Wrong Door

*3rd-level conjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a key that fits no lock)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You touch a doorway (a door, gate, arch, hatch or other frame no larger than 10 feet on a side) and name another doorway you have seen within 500 feet. For the duration, the two are linked both ways: anything that passes through one comes out of the other. Opening or closing the doors doesn't break the link.

When you cast the spell, roll a d20. On a 1, the link opens onto another doorway you have seen within 500 feet, chosen by the DM.

**At Higher Levels.** With a 4th-level slot the range becomes 1 mile, and with a 5th-level slot it becomes 10 miles.

***Margin.*** *"rolled the 1 twice. both times, a privy. not the same privy."*

### Local Forecast

*3rd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Self (300-foot radius)\
**Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of salt and a feather)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

You must be outdoors. Choose a weather; it arrives over the area within 1 minute, and you can change it as an action (the new weather also takes 1 minute to arrive):

- **Fog.** The area beyond 30 feet of any creature is heavily obscured.
- **Downpour.** Unprotected flames go out. After 1 minute, open ground becomes difficult terrain with mud. Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight or hearing have disadvantage.
- **Gale.** Ranged weapon attacks have disadvantage, and fog, smoke and gas are blown away.
- **Still Air.** No wind at all. Fog, smoke and gas linger, and sound carries: Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing have advantage.
- **Dry Heat.** Puddles dry up, mud hardens into normal ground after 1 minute, and flammable things catch fire more easily.

The spell has no effect inside the Everstorm.

***Margin.*** *"the Everstorm makes its own weather. it does not take requests."*

### Which Way Is Down

*3rd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a plumb line)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Choose a 20-foot cube within range, and one of its six faces. For the duration, gravity inside the cube pulls toward that face. You can't change the direction after casting.

When the spell takes effect, and when a creature first enters the cube, each creature in it makes a Dexterity saving throw. On a success, it grabs hold of something and doesn't fall, and it can move by climbing at half speed. On a failure, it falls toward the chosen face, taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage per 10 feet fallen and landing prone. Unattended objects fall too.

When the spell ends, normal gravity returns, and creatures and objects fall again.

***Margin.*** *"cast it on a tavern ceiling once. they still haven't got the soup off."*

## Civic Workings

*Workings of the city, kept at the back of the primer.*

### Glow Test

*Divination cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature. If it has crystal affinity, it flares briefly with the colour of its path, visible to everyone who can see it: green (Verdant), crimson (Warbound), amber (Stonewarden), violet (Hellbound), prismatic black (Sanguine) or white (Aether). If it has affinity but no path, it glows faintly without colour. If it has none, it stays dull.

An unwilling creature can make a Charisma saving throw to stay dull. Either way, it knows it was tested.

***Margin.*** *"rude at parties. done at parties constantly."*

### Receipt

*1st-level divination (ritual)*

**Casting Time:** 1 minute\
**Range:** Touch\
**Components:** V, S, M (a copper coin)\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch an object and learn the last three times it changed hands for payment: what each buyer and seller looked like, the price, and roughly where. If it changed hands some other way (stolen, made, found, gifted), you learn that instead, with no further detail.

***Margin.*** *"coins are thread; oaths are cloth. receipts are evidence."*

### Not My Department

*1st-level enchantment*

**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when a creature you can see within 30 feet of you targets you with an attack, before the roll\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S (a dismissive wave)\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

The attacker must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it must choose a different target within its reach or range, of its choice. If there is no other target, it attacks you as normal. Creatures immune to being charmed are unaffected.

***Margin.*** *"have you tried asking him?"*

### Red Tape

*2nd-level conjuration*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 60 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a length of red ribbon)\
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

Glowing red tape wraps a creature you can see within range. It must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be restrained for the duration. As an action, the restrained creature can either:

- make a Strength (Athletics) check against your spell save DC, freeing itself on a success; or
- **sign itself out**, which frees it automatically at the end of its next turn, once the paperwork goes through.

**At Higher Levels.** You can target one additional creature for each slot level above 2nd.

***Margin.*** *"signing out is faster. nobody believes that, which is the joke."*

### Overtime

*3rd-level transmutation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action\
**Range:** 30 feet\
**Components:** V, S, M (a sand-timer)\
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You and up to five willing creatures within range gain the benefits of a short rest. Each of you still has to eat the ration a short rest requires. A creature can't benefit from this spell again until it finishes a long rest.

The hour is owed. Once in the next 24 hours, at a moment the DM chooses outside combat, each creature that benefited falls asleep for 1 minute. It wakes early if it takes damage or someone uses an action to shake it awake.

***Margin.*** *"the hour is collected in Thal'vireth. with interest, they say."*
