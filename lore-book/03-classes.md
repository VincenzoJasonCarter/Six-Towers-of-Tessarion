# Classes

# **⚔️ The Three Pillars of Human Power in Tessarion**

Humans in Tessarion are divided into three core classes: **Crystal Mages** (attuned to Soulstone), **Aegisbound** (protected by magic-resistant armor), and **Range** (augmented by technology and munitions).

---

## **I. CRYSTAL MAGES (MAGE-TYPE CLASSES)**

Humans have a natural affinity for mana crystals—soulstone formed from the dead of each region. Prolonged exposure and ritual attunement awaken different paths.

### **1\. Verdant Mage (Elven Crystals – Green/Teal)**

* **Crystal:** Elven soulstone from Thal’veren, verdant green/teal.  
* **Region Flavor:** Forests, memory, elegance, old magic.  
* **Theme:** Nature control, illusions, battlefield manipulation, perception/time “feel.”  
* **Typical Powers (conceptual):** Twisting roots and vines to restrain foes. Bending light and sound to create illusions or cover. Heightening allies’ senses and reflexes. Reading emotional echoes from places or objects.  
* **Drawback:** Verdant mana pushes you toward detachment and superiority; you risk seeing others as short-lived pawns.

### **2\. Warbound Mage (Orc Crystals – Crimson)**

* **Crystal:** Orc soulstone from the Iron Hordes, burning crimson.  
* **Region Flavor:** Badlands, war, drums, scars, rage.  
* **Theme:** Lifeforce as fuel, explosive damage, melee battle-mage, buffs.  
* **Typical Powers (conceptual):** Wreathing your body or weapon in crimson aura to hit harder. Shockwaves, charges, and brutal short-range bursts. Turning your own HP into temporary boosts for damage/strength. War cries that frighten foes or rally allies.  
* **Drawback:** Crystals stoke aggression; you risk losing control, harming allies, or becoming addicted to violence.

### **3\. Stonewarden Mage (Dwarven Crystals – Amber/Gold)**

* **Crystal:** Dwarven soulstone from Khazrund, amber/gold.  
* **Region Flavor:** Deep halls, ore, tradition, stubbornness.  
* **Theme:** Wards, barriers, runes, constructs, pure durability.  
* **Typical Powers (conceptual):** Summoning shields or walls of stone or hardlight. Creating runic circles that protect or enhance allies. Animating small golems/constructs as helpers or blockers. Reinforcing armor, weapons, and structures.  
* **Drawback:** Mentally calcifies you—rigidity, obsession with “the correct way,” difficulty changing plans or beliefs.

### **4\. Hellbound Mage (Tiefling Crystals – Violet)**

* **Crystal:** Tiefling soulstone from Emberfall, violet/purplish.  
* **Region Flavor:** Ashlands, infernal pacts, guilt, ambition, intrigue.  
* **Theme:** Curses, shadow, manipulation, soul-binding.  
* **Typical Powers (conceptual):** Hexing enemies (lowering hit chance, damage, luck). Shadowstepping through dim light or smoke. Calling minor infernal spirits or “contracts” that trade HP/soul-fragments for power. Feeding on fear or despair to fuel spells.  
* **Drawback:** Crystals whisper—easy to slide into pacts and debts with unseen forces; morally slippery path.

### **5\. Sanguine Mage (Sangrith Crystals – Prismatic Black)**

* **Crystal:** Sangrith-forged prismatic black soulstone, infused with blood from all four outer races.  
* **Region Flavor:** Obsidian Crown, apex predators, blood-alchemy, control.  
* **Theme:** Hybrid/multi-aspect magic, soul manipulation, meta-magic.  
* **Typical Powers (conceptual):** Limited access to elements of the other paths (nature, war, wards, curses). Direct lifeforce manipulation (healing, draining, or rewriting). Raising “echoes” of the dead as memory phantoms, not full undead. Twisting or corrupting the mana crystals of others (suppressing or overcharging them).	  
* **Drawback:** Extremely corruptive and addictive. Overuse risks mutation, loss of self, or becoming a Sangrith thrall. Often illegal/forbidden.

### **6\. Aether Mage (Human Crystals – White/Clear) – Optional**

* **Crystal:** Neutral aetherite formed from mixed or “unclaimed” souls at the Crossroads.  
* **Region Flavor:** Hexaspire, trade, balance, adaptation.  
* **Theme:** Purification, balance, meta-support, weak mimicry of other paths.  
* **Typical Powers (conceptual):** Temporarily mimicking weaker versions of other crystal effects. Cleansing corruption from other crystals, people, and places. Stabilizing reality: cancelling or dampening chaotic magic, illusions, or curses. Boosting party mana efficiency or reducing cost of others’ abilities.  
* **Drawback:** Never the strongest in any one domain; more support-focused. Long-term use risks emotional numbness and loss of strong passions.

## **II. AEGISBOUND (TANK/BRUISER CLASSES)**

Some humans cannot adapt internally to mana crystals. Attempts to attune them fail or make them sick. Instead, they become Aegisbound: warriors sealed into armor forged from soulstone dust and blood-alloy. The armor turns them into living bulwarks and magic sinks.

### **Shared Aegisbound Traits (All Paths)**

* **High physical durability and HP.**  
* **Magic-resistant armor:** Reduced incoming magic damage / better saves vs spells.  
* Powers come from the armor’s interaction with mana and blood, not from spellcasting.  
* **Socially, they’re seen as:** Human answer to other races’ innate magic and Sangrith terror. Tools of city-states, caravans, and tower councils.

The Aegisbound divide into three main orders: **Sanguine Aegis** – HP drain / sustain. **Bulwark Aegis** – pure defense. **Warden Aegis** – battlefield control.

### **1\. Sanguine Aegis (HP Drain / Sustain)**

* **Fantasy:** Walking siphons. Their armor is laced with micro-inlays of blood coins and powdered prismatic crystal. They drain life when they hit—or get hit.  
* **Theme:** Lifesteal, sustain, aggressive tank that gets stronger over a long fight.  
* **Lore:** Developed with “borrowed” Sangrith blood-alchemy. Feared and distrusted; some churches call them half-made monsters.  
* **Signature Concepts:** **Leeching Strikes:** A portion of the damage they deal returns as HP or temporary HP, especially to marked targets. **Bloodwell Armor:** The armor stores stolen vitality as charges. Spend charges to heal, gain brief damage shields, or burst of speed/strength. **Red Pact:** Can sacrifice their own HP to empower big moves, then try to steal that life back.  
* **Drawback:** If they go too long without “feeding,” the armor rebels: fatigue, whispers, or creeping bloodlust. They risk provoking fights just to feel whole.

### **2\. Bulwark Aegis (Pure Defense)**

* **Fantasy:** The wall. They step in front of whatever horror’s coming and stand there until it breaks.  
* **Theme:** Maximum mitigation, minimum damage. Perfect bodyguards and anchors.  
* **Lore:** Co-designed by humans and dwarves of Khazrund. Deepstone plates layered with human steel and runes that channel mana into the ground. Often royal guards, caravan captains, or tower wardens in Hexaspire.  
* **Signature Concepts:** **Unmoving Bastion:** When they “dig in,” they gain massive damage reduction/resistance but reduced mobility. Great for blocking chokepoints. **Intercept:** Can step into attacks aimed at allies, taking the hit but mitigating it heavily. **Mana Baffle:** Aura that weakens magic around them. **Fortress of Tessarion:** Short-duration super-form: nearly immovable, huge DR, providing cover/temporary HP to allies nearby.  
* **Drawback:** Heavy, noticeable, and not subtle. Socially seen as “tools of the state” or enforcers, not free agents. Struggles with stealth or agile playstyles.

### **3\. Warden Aegis (Control / Battlefield Lockdown)**

* **Fantasy:** They don’t just survive; they decide where everyone stands and where no one moves. Their armor is studded with anchoring crystals and chain-like conduits that twist gravity and space.  
* **Theme:** Battlefield control, zoning, soft tanking by limiting enemy options.  
* **Lore:** Developed by humans with tiefling engineers from Emberfall. Originally for riot control, mage containment, and defending critical tower sites.  
* **Signature Concepts:** **Gravitic Anchor:** Drop anchors that slow or pull enemies around them. **Lockstep Field:** Zone around them where enemies that move take damage, get slowed, or risk being knocked down. **Shackle Strikes:** Attacks that impose roots, cripples, forced movement, or restrictions. **Mana Cage:** Short-lived field that weakens or scrambles spell effects inside.  
* **Drawback:** Less raw DR than Bulwarks and less sustain than Sanguine Aegis. Needs good positioning and team coordination; enemies with teleport/flying can partially bypass their zones.

## **III. RANGE (THE AUGMENTED CLASS)**

Humans are defined by their reliance on **distance, precision, and technology** (archery and gunpowder) to deliver targeted power. They are the great **Equalizers** against world-bending foes.

### **Shared Range Traits**

* **Focus:** Distance, precision, and human craft (ballistics, engineering).  
* **Power Source:** They **infuse** Soulstone dust/crystals into their ammunition or weapon components, not their bodies.  
* **Role:** Provides highly focused, targeted damage capable of bypassing magical wards and natural resilience.

### **1\. Archers (Strikers)**

* **Core Weaponry:** Composite Bows, Crossbows, Specialized Quivers.  
* **Theme & Focus:** Versatility, silence, speed, and adaptable munitions.  
* **Signature Concepts:** **Modular Ammunition** (arrowheads coated in dust from any of the six soulstones, e.g., Verdant dust for binding or Red dust for concussive blast); **Crystal Sights** (small Amber crystals for enhanced focus/targeting).  
* **Drawback:** Less effective against heavy armor; dependent on **costly, specialized ammunition**; requires high mobility and open space.

### **2\. Gunmen (Artificers/Ballisticians)**

* **Core Weaponry:** Black-powder Rifles, Heavy Handguns, Bombards.  
* **Theme & Focus:** Kinetic force, disruption, anti-armor, and technology.  
* **Signature Concepts:** **Crystal Casing Rounds** (loading any of the six soulstones behind the shot to focus energy, e.g., Red for raw impact, Amber to crack armor, White to snuff out spells). **Powder Disruption** (black powder inherently disrupts magical entities).  
* **Drawback:** **Loud** and draws immediate attention; dependent on scarce or volatile resources (powder, specific casings); vulnerable in close quarters/melee.
