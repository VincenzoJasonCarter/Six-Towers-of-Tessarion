## **🗡️ Crude Sword**

**Damage Type:** Slashing  
**Weapon Type:** Melee, Versatile

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** STR mod \+ Proficiency  
* **Damage Roll:**  
  * One-handed: **1d6 \+ STR mod** slashing  
  * Two-handed: **1d8 \+ STR mod** slashing

### **Power Modifier — *Poor Balance***

* On a **natural 1** on an attack roll:  
   → You have **disadvantage on your next weapon attack** before the end of your next turn.

> Design intent: early melee viable, but punishes reckless multi-attack spam.

### **Recipe**

**Materials**

* Impure Ingot ×2  
* Wood Bundle ×0.5  
* Cord Bundle ×0.2

**Tools:** Smith’s tools  
 **Craft Time:** 1 day (basic forge)

## **🏹 Crude Bow**

**Damage Type:** Piercing  
 **Weapon Type:** Ranged, Ammunition, Two-Handed

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** DEX mod \+ Proficiency  
* **Damage Roll:** **1d6 \+ DEX mod** piercing

### **Power Modifier — *Inaccurate Limbs***

* Attacks made **beyond normal range** are at **disadvantage**  
   (this replaces the usual long-range rule)  
* You **cannot gain advantage** on attacks beyond normal range from features or conditions.  
* Your movement gets cut in half when attacking.  
* Design intent: forces positioning & punishes “sniper at level 1”.

### **Recipe**

**Materials**

* Wood Bundle ×1  
* Cord Bundle ×0.5

**Tools:** Carpenter’s tools or Bowyer’s kit  
**Craft Time:** 1 day

## **🛡️ Rusty Shield**

### **Defensive Profile**

* **Item Type:** Shield  
* **AC Bonus:** **\+2 AC** (while wielded)

The Rusty Shield can be used as a **melee weapon**.

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** **Strength modifier \+ Proficiency bonus**  
* **Damage Roll:** **1d4**  
* **Damage Type:** Bludgeoning

> This attack counts as an **improvised blunt weapon**, but **does not impose disadvantage** when used by a creature proficient with shields.

### **Power Modifier — *Awkward Mass***

* When you use the Rusty Shield to make a **shield bash**, you **cannot benefit from Two-Weapon Fighting** during that turn.  
* If you roll a **natural 1** on a shield bash attack, you have **disadvantage on the next Strength-based attack** you make before the end of your next turn.

This keeps shield bash usable but not spammable.

### **Defensive Modifier — Unreliable Guard**

* When you are hit by a **critical hit**, this shield provides **no AC bonus** against that attack.  
* After a combat in which you suffered a critical hit, the DM may rule the shield requires **minor repair** to avoid worsening condition.

### **Crafting Recipe**

**Core Frame:** Shield

**Materials**

* Impure Ingot ×2  
* Wood Bundle ×0.5

**Tools:** Smith’s tools  
**Craft Time:** 2 days

## **🔪 Basic Dagger**

### **Weapon Profile**

* **Weapon Type:** Simple Melee Weapon  
* **Damage Type:** Piercing or Slashing (choose on hit)  
* **Properties:** Light, Thrown (20/60)  
* **Finesse:** ❌ No

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** **Strength modifier \+ Proficiency bonus**  
* **Damage Roll:** **1d4 \+ Strength modifier**

> Dexterity **cannot** be used for attack or damage rolls with this weapon.

### **Power Modifier — *Crude Construction***

* When you make a **thrown attack** with this weapon, **do not add your Strength modifier** to the damage roll.  
* This weapon **cannot benefit from features, feats, or effects** that require a *finesse weapon*.

### **Utility Modifier — *Working Knife***

Outside combat, this dagger grants **advantage** on ability checks used to:

* skin or butcher animals  
* cut rope, leather, hides, or bindings  
* perform quick, close-range cutting tasks

This modifier provides **no combat advantage**.

### **Crafting Recipe**

**Core Frame:** Weapon (Melee)

**Materials**

* Impure Ingot ×1  
* Wood Bundle ×0.2

**Tools:** Smith’s tools  
**Craft Time:** 6 hours

 **Weapon Type:** Melee, One-Handed

## **🔨 Iron Mace**

**Weapon Profile**

* Damage Type: Bludgeoning  
* Weapon Type: Melee, Martial

**Attack & Damage**

* Attack Bonus: STR mod \+ Proficiency  
* Damage Roll: 1d6 \+ STR mod \+ 1 bludgeoning

**Power Modifier — Crushing Weight**  
 On a hit against a target wearing medium or heavy armor (or possessing natural armor granting \+2 AC or more), you deal an additional \+1 bludgeoning damage.

*Design intent: mace's real-world niche was always anti-armor. This keeps it competitive against Iron Sword/Battle Axe (same 1d6-1d8 range) without making it strictly better — the bonus only matters against armored targets, so it's dead weight against unarmored enemies, which is where Iron Dagger/Club still shine.*

**Upgrade Recipe**  
 Materials

* Iron Ingot ×1.5  
* Seasoned Timber ×0.3  
* Treated Leather Strip ×0.1

Tools: Smith's tools  
 Craft Time: 1 day

## **👢 Leather Boots**

**Armor Profile**

* Armor Type: Light Armor (Feet)  
* Armor Tier: 1  
* Material Tier: 2  
* Base AC Bonus: \+0  
* Dex Scaling: —  
* Stealth Penalty: None  
* Weight Class: Light

**Defense & Utility**  
 Effect (choose on craft):

* \+5 ft movement speed, **or**  
* Advantage on DEX saves to avoid being knocked prone

**Design Intent**  
 Completes the Tier 1 armor set (Tunic/Leggings/Hood/Boots) with a choice between mobility or stability — deliberately no AC bump, keeping it consistent with the Leggings and Hood: utility-first, not a defensive spike. All four pieces sit at equal value, differentiated by niche rather than power (body \= core protection, legs/head/feet \= minor utility choices).

**Upgrade Recipe**  
 Materials

* Cured Hide ×1  
* Binding Fiber ×1

Tools: Leatherworker's Tools  
 Craft Time: 5 hours

Craft Notes

* The chosen effect is locked in permanently once crafting is complete.  
* Craft failure rarely produces a Fractured result (same as the Hood).
