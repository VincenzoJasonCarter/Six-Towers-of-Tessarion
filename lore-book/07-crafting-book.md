## **🗡️ Crude Sword**

**Damage Type:** Slashing  
**Weapon Type:** Melee, Versatile

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** STR mod \+ Proficiency  
* **Damage Roll:**  
  * One-handed: **1d6 \+ STR mod** slashing  
  * Two-handed: **1d8 \+ STR mod** slashing

### **Power Modifier — *Poor Balance***

* On a **natural 1** on an attack roll:  
   → You have **disadvantage on your next weapon attack** before the end of your next turn.

> Design intent: early melee viable, but punishes reckless multi-attack spam.

### **Recipe**

**Materials**

* Impure Ingot ×2  
* Wood Bundle ×0.5  
* Cord Bundle ×0.2

**Tools:** Smith’s tools  
 **Craft Time:** 1 day (basic forge)

## **🏹 Crude Bow**

**Damage Type:** Piercing  
 **Weapon Type:** Ranged, Ammunition, Two-Handed

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** DEX mod \+ Proficiency  
* **Damage Roll:** **1d6 \+ DEX mod** piercing

### **Power Modifier — *Inaccurate Limbs***

* Attacks made **beyond normal range** are at **disadvantage**  
   (this replaces the usual long-range rule)  
* You **cannot gain advantage** on attacks beyond normal range from features or conditions.  
* Your movement gets cut in half when attacking.  
* Design intent: forces positioning & punishes “sniper at level 1”.

### **Recipe**

**Materials**

* Wood Bundle ×1  
* Cord Bundle ×0.5

**Tools:** Carpenter’s tools or Bowyer’s kit

## **🛡️ Rusty Shield**

### **Defensive Profile**

* **Item Type:** Shield  
* **AC Bonus:** **\+2 AC** (while wielded)

The Rusty Shield can be used as a **melee weapon**.

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** **Strength modifier \+ Proficiency bonus**  
* **Damage Roll:** **1d4**  
* **Damage Type:** Bludgeoning

> This attack counts as an **improvised blunt weapon**, but **does not impose disadvantage** when used by a creature proficient with shields.

### **Power Modifier — *Awkward Mass***

* When you use the Rusty Shield to make a **shield bash**, you **cannot benefit from Two-Weapon Fighting** during that turn.  
* If you roll a **natural 1** on a shield bash attack, you have **disadvantage on the next Strength-based attack** you make before the end of your next turn.

This keeps shield bash usable but not spammable.

### **Defensive Modifier — Unreliable Guard**

* When you are hit by a **critical hit**, this shield provides **no AC bonus** against that attack.  
* After a combat in which you suffered a critical hit, the DM may rule the shield requires **minor repair** to avoid worsening condition.

### **Crafting Recipe**

**Core Frame:** Shield

**Materials**

* Impure Ingot ×2  
* Wood Bundle ×0.5

**Tools:** Smith’s tools  
**Craft Time:** 2 days

## **🔪 Basic Dagger**

* **Weapon Type:** Simple Melee Weapon  
* **Damage Type:** Piercing or Slashing (choose on hit)

### **Power Modifier — *Crude Construction***

* When you make a **thrown attack** with this weapon, **do not add your Strength modifier** to the damage roll.

### **Utility Modifier — *Working Knife***

Outside combat, this dagger grants **advantage** on ability checks used to:

* skin or butcher animals  
* cut rope, leather, hides, or bindings  
* perform quick, close-range cutting tasks

This modifier provides **no combat advantage**.

* Impure Ingot ×1  
* Wood Bundle ×0.2

**Tools:** Smith’s tools  
**Craft Time:** 6 hours

## **🔫 Matchlock Musket**

**Damage Type:** Piercing
**Weapon Type:** Firearm, Ranged (40/120), Ammunition (shot and powder), Loading, Two-Handed, Loud

### **Attack & Damage**

* **Attack Bonus:** DEX mod \+ Proficiency
* **Damage Roll:** **1d12 \+ DEX mod** piercing

### **Power Modifier — *Misfire***

* On a **natural 1**, the charge fizzles and fouls the barrel: you can’t fire it again until you use an **action** to clear it. The round is spent.

### **Property — *Loud***

* Every shot is heard by anything within **300 ft**, and gives away where it came from.

> Design intent: the Gunman’s rifle at its crudest (Classes, Range). The biggest die in the book, paid for with Loading, noise, a fouled barrel on a bad roll, and powder that costs twice what arrows do. A Gunman’s Ballistic Training ignores Loading.

> **Pricing:** firearms are priced by what they do, not by their materials. Flint-and-Steel’s craft is scarce and Guild-sealed, so a gun costs about twice a melee weapon of like damage (the Matchlock Musket against the Crude Greatsword).

### **Recipe**

**Materials**

* Impure Ingot ×3 (barrel)
* Wood Bundle ×1 (stock)
* Cord Bundle ×0.5 (slow match)

**Tools:** Smith’s tools and Tinker’s tools
**Craft Time:** 3 days (basic forge)
**Market Value:** 6 CB

## **🧨 Black Powder (10 charges)**

**Raw Material** (Tier 2)

* Refined at **Flint-and-Steel** in Cleoti, Northreach, and sold under Guild seal elsewhere; outside Northreach it counts as **scarce** at a local market.

# **TIER 2 ARMOR**

---

Tier 2 armor is **Medium**: the Tier 1 leather set riveted with iron, and the body piece plated. Each piece is an upgrade of its Tier 1 piece and **keeps the effect chosen when the Tier 1 piece was crafted**, strengthened; the choice is still never remade. Only the body piece adds Armor Class, as at Tier 1.

**The Medium drawback sits on the body piece alone.** A character who needs to stay quiet can keep the Leather Tunic and still wear Tier 2 leggings, coif and boots: one point of AC traded for silence. A character counts as wearing medium armor (for the Iron Mace’s Crushing Weight, among others) only while their body piece is Medium.

**Armor is priced by the protection it gives, not by its materials.** The Guild values it on the same footing as the old weapon-and-armor price lists, where a longsword (the Iron Sword) costs about a quarter of a set of scale mail. Materials stay cheap, so a leatherworker who makes their own saves most of the price, for a day’s work and the risk of a Fractured result.

**Design Intent**  
Iron plates riveted between leather and padding. One more point of AC than the Leather Tunic, the same step the Tier 2 weapons take, paid for in noise. The plates turn the worst blow of a fight rather than every blow, echoing the Iron Shield’s Rigid Guard.

# **TIER 2 UTILITIES**

---

Tier 1 utilities (Rations, Rope, the Compression Lighter) are bought, not made; see the Official Tessarion Market Value. Their Tier 2 counterparts are what a party reaches for once the road gets longer, wetter and darker. Like Tier 2 weapons, each improves on its Tier 1 item rather than replacing what it does.

## **🥫 Crucible Tinned Rations (4 uses)**

*(Upgraded from Rations)*

**Utility Profile**

* Item Type: Consumable  
* Uses: 4 (each use is a day’s ration)

**Effect**

* Sealed tins from the Crucible’s preserving-houses, cooked on the Vessels’ waste heat.  
* They **never spoil**, and survive soaking, fire and being sat on.  
* Each use counts as a day’s ration for every rule that asks for one, exactly as Rations do.

**Design Intent**  
 Costs the same per use as Rations, so it never makes resting cheaper; what it buys is certainty on long voyages and in bad weather.

**Market Value:** 2 CB

**Craft Notes**

* Not craftable by hand: the tins are sealed in the Crucible’s preserving-houses.

# **TOOLS & THE WORKSHOP**

---

Every recipe in this book names the tools it is worked with; without them in hand, it can’t be worked. The Guild prices tools on the same footing as armor and weapons (a set of smith’s tools costs a little more than an Iron Sword). Tools are not tiered: the same kit works Tier 1 and Tier 2 recipes alike.

## **🔨 Smith’s Tools**

* Hammers, tongs, a set of punches and a hardy.
* Works every recipe that calls for a **forge**, together with **Forge Hire** (or a forge of your own).
* **Market Value:** 5 CB

## **🧵 Leatherworker’s Tools**

* Knives, awls, an edger and a stitching pony.
* Works the leather armor, the Riveted pieces (with Smith’s tools for the Brigandine), and leather fittings.
* **Market Value:** 1 CB

## **🪚 Carpenter’s Tools**

* Saw, adze, planes, chisels.
* Works staves, clubs, bows and crossbows.
* **Market Value:** 2 CB

## **🏹 Bowyer’s Kit**

* Tillering stick, rasps, a stringing board.
* Works bows and crossbows, in place of Carpenter’s tools.
* **Market Value:** 2 CB

## **🪶 Fletcher’s Tools**

* Fletching jig, glue pot, a small knife.
* Works arrows and bolts.
* **Market Value:** 1 CB

## **⚙️ Tinker’s Tools**

* Files, small hammers, a vice and a hand drill: the gunsmith’s kit.
* Works locks, lighters, lanterns, firearms and their cartridges.
* **Market Value:** 13 CB

## **🧶 Weaver’s Tools**

* Shuttles, a ropewalk hook and a twisting wheel.
* Works rope and cord.
* **Market Value:** 1 CB

## **🔥 Forge Hire (1 day)**

* A day at a chartered forge: fire, anvil and quench. Recipes marked *(basic forge)* need one.
* Bring your own Smith’s tools.
* **Market Value:** 1 CB per day
