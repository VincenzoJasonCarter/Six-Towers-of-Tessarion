# Subclasses Skill

## **WIZARD ARCANE TRADITIONS – CRYSTAL MAGES**

### **Verdant Mage (Arcane Tradition)**

**Verdant Attunement**  
 *2nd-level Verdant Mage feature*  
 When you choose this tradition at 2nd level, your bond to elven crystal awakens.

* You gain proficiency in **Nature** or **Perception** (your choice).  
* You learn the **druidcraft** cantrip. It counts as a wizard cantrip for you.  
* You learn one additional 1st-level spell: **entangle** or **faerie fire**. It counts as a wizard spell for you, and you can cast that chosen spell **once without expending a spell slot**. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Rootbind**  
 *2nd-level Verdant Mage feature*  
 You learn a special verdant cantrip, **rootbind**.

* **Casting Time:** 1 action  
* **Range:** 30 feet  
* **Target:** One creature you can see within range  
* **Saving Throw:** Strength saving throw

On a failed save, the target’s speed is **reduced by 10 feet** until the start of your next turn.

At 5th level, and again at 11th and 17th level, you can affect **one additional creature** within 5 feet of the original target.

---

**Memory of the Grove**  
 *6th-level Verdant Mage feature*  
 Your connection to old forests sharpens your and your allies’ senses.

When you or a creature you can see within 30 feet of you:

* fails a **Wisdom (Perception)** check, or

* fails a saving throw against being **charmed** or **frightened**,

you can use your **reaction** to allow that creature to **reroll** the check or saving throw. It must use the new roll.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Intelligence modifier** (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Verdant Veil**  
 *10th-level Verdant Mage feature*  
 While you are not wearing medium or heavy armor, you gain a **\+1 bonus to AC**.

In addition, as a **bonus action**, you can choose yourself or one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. Until the start of your next turn, **ranged weapon attacks** made from more than 10 feet away against the chosen creature are made with **disadvantage**.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Master of Living Paths**  
 *14th-level Verdant Mage feature*  
 You move with the will of the wild.

* **Difficult terrain** composed of plants doesn’t cost you or creatures you choose within 10 feet of your extra movement.

* When you cast a spell that **restrains a creature** or **reduces a creature’s speed**, you can choose one affected creature; that creature has **disadvantage** on the **first saving throw** it makes against that spell.

* Once per turn, when a creature fails a saving throw against one of your spells that restrains or reduces its speed, you can **teleport up to 15 feet** to an unoccupied space you can see.

---

### **Warbound Mage (Arcane Tradition)**

**Crimson Attunement**  
 *2nd-level Warbound Mage feature*  
 Your soul resonates with orcish war-crystals.

* You gain proficiency in **Athletics** or **Intimidation** (your choice).  
* You learn one of the following cantrips: **green-flame blade** or **booming blade**. It counts as a wizard cantrip for you.

* You learn one additional 1st-level spell: **shield** or **hellish rebuke**. It counts as a wizard spell for you, and you can cast the chosen spell **once without expending a spell slot**. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Battle Surge**  
 *2nd-level Warbound Mage feature*  
 Your magic feeds on the clash of steel.

Once on each of your turns, when you:

* reduce a creature to 0 hit points, or  
* score a **critical hit** with an attack or spell,

you gain **temporary hit points** equal to your **Intelligence modifier \+ your wizard level** (minimum of 1).

---

**Blood for Power**  
 *6th-level Warbound Mage feature*  
 You can burn your lifeforce to empower your spells.

As a **bonus action**, you can lose a number of hit points up to your **wizard level** (you can’t reduce yourself below 1 hit point this way). Until the end of the current turn, you add **half the hit points lost (rounded up)** to the **damage rolls** of your wizard spells.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Crimson Presence**  
 *10th-level Warbound Mage feature*  
 Your mere presence can turn the tide of battle.

* Allies of your choice within **10 feet** of you have **advantage on saving throws** against being **frightened**.

* When you cast a spell that deals damage, you can choose one of the spell’s targets. That target has **disadvantage** on the **first saving throw** it makes against that spell.

You can use the second part of this feature a number of times equal to your **Intelligence modifier** (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Warstorm**  
 *14th-level Warbound Mage feature*  
 You can enter a state of raging spellcraft.

As an **action**, you can enter a warstorm state for **1 minute**. While it lasts:

* When you deal damage with a wizard spell, you can add your **Intelligence modifier** to **one damage roll** of that spell.

* When a hostile creature within 10 feet of you drops to 0 hit points, you can use your **reaction** to move up to **half your speed** without provoking opportunity attacks.

* Each time you cast a spell of 1st level or higher, you take **1d6 psychic damage**.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.

---

### **Stonewarden Mage (Arcane Tradition)**

**Deepstone Attunement**  
 *2nd-level Stonewarden Mage feature*  
 You forge a bond with dwarven soulstone.

* You gain proficiency in **History** or with **smith’s tools** (your choice).

* You learn the **mold earth** cantrip. It counts as a wizard cantrip for you.

* You learn one additional 1st-level spell: **mage armor** or **absorb elements**. It counts as a wizard spell for you, and you can cast the chosen spell **once without expending a spell slot**. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Stoneguard**  
 *2nd-level Stonewarden Mage feature*  
 You encase yourself in subtle, crystalline armor.

* While you are **not wearing medium or heavy armor**, your base AC becomes **13 \+ your Dexterity modifier**.

* If you are under the effect of **mage armor**, your AC is instead increased by an additional **\+1**.

---

**Runic Bulwark**  
 *6th-level Stonewarden Mage feature*  
 You inscribe fleeting wards in the air.

When you or a creature you can see within **30 feet** of you takes damage, you can use your **reaction** to grant that creature **resistance** to that instance of damage.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Intelligence modifier** (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Living Rampart**  
 *10th-level Stonewarden Mage feature*  
 You stand like a carved pillar.

* You count as **one size larger** when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift, and when resisting being **pushed** or **knocked prone**.

* When you cast a spell that creates a **wall, barrier, or other protective effect**, you gain **temporary hit points** equal to your **Intelligence modifier** (minimum of 1).

---

**Stoneheart Aegis**  
 *14th-level Stonewarden Mage feature*  
 You can anchor an entire battlefield.

As an **action**, you create a **30-foot-radius aura** centered on you that lasts for **1 minute**. While the aura persists:

* Allies of your choice in the aura gain a **\+2 bonus to AC** and **advantage on Constitution saving throws**.

* Once per round, when a creature within 30 feet of you is targeted by an attack, you can use your **reaction** to **teleport up to 10 feet** to an unoccupied space adjacent to that creature and become the target of the attack instead.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.

---

### **Hellbound Mage (Arcane Tradition)**

**Hellglass Attunement**  
 *2nd-level Hellbound Mage feature*  
 Infernal echoes resonate through your crystal.

* You gain proficiency in **Deception** or **Insight** (your choice).

* You learn the **thaumaturgy** cantrip. It counts as a wizard cantrip for you.

* You learn one additional 1st-level spell: **hex** or **bane**. It counts as a wizard spell for you, and you can cast the chosen spell **once without expending a spell slot**. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Shadow Mark**  
 *2nd-level Hellbound Mage feature*  
 You can mark enemies for torment.

As a **bonus action**, you can mark one creature you can see within 60 feet of you. Until the end of your next turn, the **first time** that creature fails a saving throw against one of your spells, it takes additional **necrotic damage equal to your Intelligence modifier** (minimum of 1).

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Curseweaver**  
 *6th-level Hellbound Mage feature*  
 You twist misfortune like a knife.

When a creature affected by one of your spells **fails a saving throw**, you can use your **reaction** to impose one of the following additional effects on that creature:

* Its speed is **reduced by 10 feet** until the end of your next turn, or

* It has **disadvantage on the next attack roll** it makes before the end of its next turn.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Intelligence modifier** (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Veil of Emberfall**  
 *10th-level Hellbound Mage feature*  
 You thrive in shadow.

* You can **see in magical darkness** to a range of **60 feet**.

* While you are in **dim light or darkness**, ranged attacks made against you are at **disadvantage**.

* You can cast the **darkness** spell **once without expending a spell slot**. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Pact Nexus**  
 *14th-level Hellbound Mage feature*  
 You bind an area in unseen contracts.

As an **action**, you create a **20-foot-radius zone** centered on a point you can see within 60 feet. The zone lasts for **1 minute** and moves with you if you wish (no action required).

* The area is **difficult terrain** for hostile creatures.

* The first time on a turn that a hostile creature in the zone **makes an attack** or **casts a spell**, it must succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw** against your spell save DC or take **necrotic damage equal to your Intelligence modifier** (minimum of 1).

When the effect ends, you must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw** or gain **one level of exhaustion**.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.

---

### **Sanguine Mage (Arcane Tradition)**

**Omnicrystal Attunement**  
 *2nd-level Sanguine Mage feature*  
 You are attuned to the prismatic soulstone of Sangrith.

* You gain proficiency in **Arcana** or **Religion** (your choice).  
* You learn one additional wizard cantrip of your choice.  
* You learn one 1st-level spell: **inflict wounds** or **cure wounds**. It counts as a wizard spell for you, and you can cast the chosen spell **once without expending a spell slot**. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Soul Tether**  
 *2nd-level Sanguine Mage feature*  
 You can bind a creature’s essence.

When you deal damage to a creature, you can mark it as **Tethered** (only one creature can be Tethered by you at a time). The tether lasts for **3 turns**, or until the target drops to 0 hit points, or until you Tether a different creature.

While a creature is Tethered, you can use a **bonus action** to deal **1d6 necrotic damage** to it and regain hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt.

You can use this bonus action a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Flux Manipulation**  
 *6th-level Sanguine Mage feature*  
 You can twist the form and focus of your magic.

When you cast a spell that deals damage, you can change the spell’s damage type to **acid, cold, fire, lightning, necrotic, or psychic**.

In addition, when you cast a spell that requires a saving throw, you can change the **ability score** used for that save to another ability (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma), if the DM agrees it makes narrative sense.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Intelligence modifier** (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Prismatic Echoes**  
 *10th-level Sanguine Mage feature*  
 Your victories echo through your magic.

When a spell you cast reduces a creature to 0 hit points, you can use your **reaction** to gain **advantage** on one **attack roll, ability check, or saving throw** you make before the end of your next turn.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Sanguine Overlord**  
 *14th-level Sanguine Mage feature*  
 You momentarily rule over blood and fate.

As an **action**, you enter a prismatic blood-state for **1 minute**. While it lasts:

* When you deal spell damage to a **Large or smaller** creature, you can move it up to **10 feet** in any direction (no save).  
* When a creature within **30 feet** of you fails a saving throw against one of your spells, you can choose to **change that result**:

  * You can turn a **success into a failure**, or  
  * A **failure into a success**.  
     Each time you do so, you take **2d8 psychic damage**.

If this effect ends while you are at 0 hit points, you **automatically succeed** on your first death saving throw.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.

---

### **Aether Mage (Arcane Tradition, Optional)**

**Aether Attunement**  
 *2nd-level Aether Mage feature*  
 You are attuned to neutral aetherite.

* You gain proficiency in **Medicine** or **Insight** (your choice).

* You learn the **guidance** cantrip. It counts as a wizard cantrip for you.

* You learn one 1st-level spell: **detect magic** or **protection from evil and good**. It counts as a wizard spell for you, and you can cast the chosen spell **once without expending a spell slot**. You regain the ability to do so when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Stabilizing Pulse**  
 *2nd-level Aether Mage feature*  
 You can push your magic outward as a steadying wave.

As an **action**, choose one creature you can see within 30 feet of you. You end **one** of the following effects on that creature:

* One level of **exhaustion**  
* The **frightened** condition  
* The **charmed** condition  
* The **poisoned** condition

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **Intelligence modifier** (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Adaptive Resonance**  
 *6th-level Aether Mage feature*  
 Your magic can mimic other crystal paths.

After each **long rest**, choose one other crystal path (Verdant, Warbound, Stonewarden, Hellbound, or Sanguine). Until you finish your next long rest:

* You may treat **one spell of each level 1–5** associated with that path’s theme (DM-approved) as if it were on the **wizard spell list** for you.

* You can learn and prepare those spells as normal wizard spells.

---

**Null Field**  
 *10th-level Aether Mage feature*  
 You can create an area where magic falters.

As a **bonus action**, you create a **10-foot-radius aura** centered on you that lasts for **1 minute**. While the aura persists:

* Creatures of your choice in the aura have **advantage on saving throws against spells**.

* When a spell of **3rd level or lower** that targets only one creature within the aura is cast, you can use your **reaction** to force a **reroll** of the spell attack roll or the saving throw result (your choice).

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Anchor of Tessarion**  
 *14th-level Aether Mage feature*  
 You become a fixed point in the weave of magic.

* You gain **resistance to force and psychic damage**.  
* You can choose to **ignore any effect** that would forcibly move you or teleport you, unless you are willing.  
* Once per long rest, when a creature you can see within **30 feet** of you would **die** (drop to 0 hit points and fail death saves, or be instantly killed), you can use your **reaction** to cause that creature to drop to **1 hit point instead**. When you do so, you gain **one level of exhaustion**.

## **MARTIAL ARCHETYPES – AEGISBOUND**

### **Sanguine Aegis (Martial Archetype)**

**Bloodwell Armor**  
 *3rd-level Sanguine Aegis feature*  
 Your armor is etched with channels that store stolen vitality.

When you hit a creature with a **melee weapon attack**, you gain a number of **Blood Charges** equal to **the damage dealt ÷ 10** (round down, minimum of 1).

* You can have a maximum number of Blood Charges equal to your **Constitution modifier \+ your proficiency bonus** (minimum of 1).

As a **bonus action**, you can spend any number of Blood Charges. For each charge you spend, choose one:

* You regain **1d4 hit points**, or

* You gain **1d4 temporary hit points**.

---

**Leeching Strikes**  
 *3rd-level Sanguine Aegis feature*  
 Your blows draw strength from your enemies.

Once on each of your turns, when you deal damage to a creature with a **melee weapon attack**, you regain **hit points equal to your proficiency bonus**.

---

**Red Pact**  
 *7th-level Sanguine Aegis feature*  
 Your armor answers the call of war.

When you roll **initiative** and have **no Blood Charges**, you immediately gain Blood Charges equal to your **proficiency bonus**.

In addition, as a **bonus action**, you can spend up to **a number of Blood Charges equal to half your fighter level** (round down):

* You add the number of charges spent to **one weapon damage roll** this turn.

* The target of that attack must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw** (DC \= 8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your Constitution modifier) or become **frightened of you** until the end of your next turn.

---

**Hunger of the Armor**  
 *10th-level Sanguine Aegis feature*  
 The armor’s thirst shapes your mind.

At the end of your turn, if you **haven’t hit a creature** with an attack since your last turn, you must succeed on a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or have **disadvantage on your next attack roll or ability check** that you make before the end of your next turn.

When you reduce a creature to **0 hit points**, you can choose to end this penalty early and gain **temporary hit points equal to twice your proficiency bonus**.

---

**Sanguine Overdrive**  
 *15th-level Sanguine Aegis feature*  
 You can unleash the full hunger of your armor.

As a **bonus action**, you can enter a sanguine overdrive for **1 minute**. While this form lasts:

* At the **start of each of your turns**, you gain **1 Blood Charge**.

* Your melee weapon attacks deal an extra **1d8 necrotic damage**.

* When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points, you **regain one expended Hit Die**.

When this form ends, you gain **one level of exhaustion**.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.

---

### **Bulwark Aegis (Martial Archetype)**

**Living Wall**  
 *3rd-level Bulwark Aegis feature*  
 You are trained to become an impenetrable barrier.

* While you are wearing **heavy armor**, you gain a **\+1 bonus to AC**.

* When a creature you can see makes an attack against a target other than you that is within **5 feet** of you, you can use your **reaction** to impose **disadvantage** on that attack roll.

---

**Anchored Stance**  
 *3rd-level Bulwark Aegis feature*  
 You can turn yourself into a rooted bastion.

If you move **no more than 10 feet** on your turn while wearing armor, you gain **resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical weapons** until the start of your next turn.

---

**Unmoving Bastion**  
 *7th-level Bulwark Aegis feature*  
 Your presence reinforces those around you.

* While you are wearing **heavy armor**, allies of your choice within **10 feet** of you gain a **\+1 bonus to AC**.  
* Also while you are wearing heavy armor, you cannot be **pushed, knocked prone, or otherwise moved against your will**, unless you are **unconscious**.

---

**Intercepting Guard**  
 *10th-level Bulwark Aegis feature*  
 You can throw yourself into danger for others.

When a creature you can see within **10 feet** of you would take damage, you can use your **reaction** to move up to **half your speed** to an unoccupied space adjacent to that creature. You become the **target of the attack or effect instead**, and you have **resistance** to the damage dealt by that attack or effect.

---

**Fortress of Tessarion**  
 *15th-level Bulwark Aegis feature*  
 You can become a walking fortress.

As an **action**, you enter a fortress stance for **1 minute**. While it lasts:

* Your **speed becomes 0** and can’t be increased by any means.  
* You gain **resistance to all damage**.  
* Allies of your choice within **10 feet** of you have **half cover** (+2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws).

When this effect ends, you **can’t take reactions** until the start of your next turn.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.

---

### **Warden Aegis (Martial Archetype)**

**Gravitic Anchor**  
 *3rd-level Warden Aegis feature*  
 You can lock down an area of the battlefield.

As a **bonus action**, you place an invisible **anchor** in an unoccupied space within **20 feet** of you. The anchor lasts for **1 minute** or until you place another anchor.

* The area within **10 feet** of the anchor is **difficult terrain for your enemies**.  
* A creature that is your enemy and starts its turn in this area must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** (DC \= 8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your Strength modifier) or have its speed become **0 until the start of its next turn**.

---

**Shackle Strike**  
 *3rd-level Warden Aegis feature*  
 Your blows drag at your foes’ movement.

When you hit a creature with a **melee weapon attack**, you can force its speed to be **reduced by 10 feet** until the start of your next turn.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Lockstep Field**  
 *7th-level Warden Aegis feature*  
 Getting near you is easy; leaving is not.

* Spaces within **5 feet** of you are **difficult terrain for your enemies**.

* When a creature within 5 feet of you **moves out of your reach**, you can make an **opportunity attack** against it. On a hit, the creature’s speed becomes **0 for the rest of its turn**.

---

**Mana Cage**  
 *10th-level Warden Aegis feature*  
 You can warp the battlefield against spellcasters.

As an **action**, you create a **15-foot-radius dome** centered on yourself that lasts for **1 minute** or until you dismiss it (no action required). The dome moves with you.

* **Ranged spell attacks** that target creatures inside the dome are made with **disadvantage**.

* When a spell is cast from outside the dome that targets a creature inside it, the caster must make a **spellcasting ability check** against a DC of **8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your Strength or Constitution modifier** (your choice). On a **failed check**, the spell **fails** and has no effect.

You can use this feature once per **short or long rest**.

---

**Field Commander**  
 *15th-level Warden Aegis feature*  
 Your presence shapes both allies and enemies.

* Allies within **10 feet** of you can use **your Strength or Constitution modifier** (their choice) in place of their own for **Strength or Constitution saving throws**.  
* When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can move it up to **5 feet** to an unoccupied space adjacent to its original position. This movement doesn’t require a saving throw.

---

## **MARTIAL ARCHETYPES – RANGE**

### **Crystal Archer (Martial Archetype)**

**Marksman’s Focus**  
 *3rd-level Crystal Archer feature*  
 You are trained to fire with flawless precision.

* You gain proficiency with the **longbow** and **hand crossbow** if you don’t already have it.

* When you make a ranged weapon attack with a bow or crossbow on your turn and you have moved **no more than 10 feet** that turn, you deal an additional **\+2 damage** on that attack.

---

**Modular Ammunition**  
 *3rd-level Crystal Archer feature*  
 You can prepare specialized soulstone-tipped arrows.

During a **short or long rest**, you can craft a number of **special arrows** equal to your **proficiency bonus**. Each arrow is imbued with one of the following effects (you choose per arrow):

* **Verdant Dust.** On a hit, the target’s speed is **reduced by 10 feet** until the start of your next turn.

* **Red Dust.** On a hit, the attack deals an extra **1d4 thunder damage**. The target must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** (DC \= 8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your Dexterity modifier) or be **pushed 5 feet** away from you.

Any unused special arrows **lose their power** at the end of your next **long rest**.

---

**Crystal Sight**  
 *7th-level Crystal Archer feature*  
 Your eyes lock onto distant targets with supernatural clarity.

As a **bonus action**, you enter a state of hyper-focus for **1 minute**. While this state lasts:

* The **normal range** of bows you wield is **doubled**.

* You **ignore half and three-quarters cover** when making ranged weapon attacks with bows.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus**, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a **long rest**.

---

**Silent Volley**  
 *10th-level Crystal Archer feature*  
 Your arrows seem to come from nowhere.

When you take the **Attack action** on your turn and hit a creature with a ranged weapon attack:

* That creature must succeed on a **Wisdom saving throw** (DC \= 8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your Dexterity modifier) or be unable to **accurately determine your position** until the start of your next turn, unless you are plainly visible in the open. It knows the general direction, but not your exact spot.

In addition, your ranged weapon attacks with bows **no longer suffer disadvantage at long range**.

---

**Rain of Shards**  
 *15th-level Crystal Archer feature*  
 You can unleash a storm of crystalline death.

As an **action**, you fire a volley of arrows into a point you can see within your weapon’s range, creating a **20-foot-radius** area of lethal shards.

* Each creature in that area must make a **Dexterity saving throw**.  
* On a failed save, a creature takes **4d8 piercing damage plus 2d8 damage** of your choice of **force, thunder, or necrotic**  
* On a successful save, the creature takes **half** as much damage.

Allies in the area can use their **reaction** to **drop prone**, gaining **advantage** on this saving throw.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.

---

### **Gunman (Martial Archetype)**

**Ballistic Training**  
 *3rd-level Gunman feature*  
 You specialize in firearms.

* You gain proficiency with **firearms** (your DM provides statistics for these weapons).

* When you take the **Attack action** and attack with a firearm you are proficient with, you ignore the **loading** property of that weapon for that action.

---

**Crystal-Cased Rounds**  
 *3rd-level Gunman feature*  
 You infuse ammunition with soulstone effects.

During a **short or long rest**, you can craft a number of **special shots** equal to your **proficiency bonus**. Each shot is imbued with one of the following effects (you choose per shot):

* **Red Impact.** On a hit, the attack deals an extra **1d6 force damage**.

* **Violet Hex.** On a hit, the target suffers a **–1d4 penalty** to the **next saving throw** it makes before the start of your next turn.

A creature can be under the effect of **only one** special shot at a time; if it is hit by another special shot before the first effect is used, you decide which effect remains.

---

**Powder Disruption**  
 *7th-level Gunman feature*  
 Your shots rip through concentration and spellcasting.

* When you hit a creature that is **concentrating on a spell** with a firearm attack, that creature has **disadvantage** on the resulting **Constitution saving throw** to maintain concentration.

* In addition, once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with one of your **special shots**, that creature must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw** (DC \= 8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your Dexterity modifier) or be unable to **cast spells with verbal or somatic components** until the start of your next turn.

---

**Recoil Step**  
 *10th-level Gunman feature*  
 You ride the kick of your own weapon.

When you fire a firearm as part of the **Attack action** or as a **reaction**, you can move up to **10 feet** immediately after the shot is resolved. This movement **doesn’t provoke opportunity attacks**.

If you move in a **straight line** with this movement, you also gain a **\+2 bonus to AC** until the start of your next turn.

---

**Soulshot Barrage**  
 *15th-level Gunman feature*  
 You can unleash a devastating burst of soul-infused shots.

As an **action**, you fire up to **three shots**, each at one creature you can see within your firearm’s range (you can target the same creature more than once).

* Each attack is made as a separate firearm attack.  
* On a hit, the attack deals its normal weapon damage plus **2d8 additional damage** of your choice: **force, necrotic, or thunder**.  
* A creature hit by at least one of these attacks must make a **Wisdom saving throw** (DC \= 8 \+ your proficiency bonus \+ your Dexterity modifier). On a failed save, the creature is **stunned until the start of your next turn**.

After you use this feature, the firearm used for the barrage is **inoperable** until you spend a **short rest** repairing it.

Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a **long rest**.
