## **I. OF THE STRUCTURE KNOWN AS THE VERDANT TOWER**

The Verdant Tower stands within the eastern forests of Thal’veren, in a region whose precise coordinates are withheld by joint accord.

It is not accurate to say the Tower was *built*.  
 It is not accurate to say it *grew*.  
 Both explanations fail upon closer inspection.

The forest does not encroach upon the Tower.  
 The forest is **arranged** around it.

No foundation trench, quarry scar, or transport record has ever been authenticated.

## **II. ON FORM, SUBSTANCE, AND MEASUREMENT**

The outer body presents as pale stone threaded seamlessly with living arboreal matter. Roots act as load-bearing structures. Stone exhibits vein-like branching.

Recorded measurements taken at different times do not reconcile.

Interior passages have been observed to realign subtly between sanctioned entries.

This effect is **publicly attributed** to ambient verdant mana saturation.
